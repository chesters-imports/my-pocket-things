# SKYLINE CU — MORNING WIRE

Press District minutes, Thursday.

Before dawn the sister desk printed a forgotten god found underfoot — scans finished their embarrassing length and located the charge where it had always been. Vessel unnamed. Calendar-split night cited. This desk declines to stamp the vessel. We stamp the minutes: he was already here.

Elsewhere the lobby put on a hundred doors and still no way out, and the day blotter rolled to Thursday while a cast page stirred with stones skipped across time.
