# The Kindness Bell

Brass. Small. Sits next to the wolf.

Ring it. The wolf tilts his head. That is the whole toy.

People buy two. Chester does not know why. He does not stop them. The second bell is for home, he guesses, where there is no wolf, which is a little sad, so the bell has to work harder.

If you ring it and nothing happens, you rang it like a customer. Ring it like a friend.
