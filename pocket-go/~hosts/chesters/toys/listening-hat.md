# The Listening Hat

A jester cap. Bells that actually work. Chester stitched the diamonds himself and then got bored halfway, which is why one triangle is hazard-stripe.

It hears the room. It will not write anything down. It is for people who want to be listened to without being remembered. The wolf thinks this is a little rude. The hat does not care. The hat is fabric.

Wear it when you are not ready for Echo Wolf. Take it off when you are.
