# The rain that fell upward, Tuesday
{{/div}}

{{div:.lib-epigraph}}
It rained all afternoon, and none of it reached the ground.
{{span:.lib-attr}}Parish register, margin note{{/span}}
{{/div}}

Between two and four o'clock the rain over Wick Lane fell upward, at the ordinary rate. Witnesses agreed it was light, steady and warm, and that umbrellas were held under the chin. No damage was reported. The gutters were found dry, and the clouds, by evening, were noticeably heavier.

The account below was returned to the desk in a jam jar, labelled in pencil. It is filed as weather, because it was weather.
