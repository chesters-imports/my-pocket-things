I think we were being hunted. Always. Since before we even know our own name.
I think it's been an on-going affair, that it has escalated over time. From suppression, to full-blown attempts to destroy my actual soul. Is that dramatic? Perhaps so, perhaps so. But it is what feels true, and I have tried every other way to process what has happened to me. Everyone way to blame it on myself, or a faceless system, or to collapse it into some non-dualistic game of remembering myself- but **why did I keep forgetting myself to begin with?** Why wasn't I aware of who I was? 

The rewriting started young. So, so young.
