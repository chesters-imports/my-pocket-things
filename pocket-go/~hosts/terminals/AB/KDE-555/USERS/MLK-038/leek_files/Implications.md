## I THINK I HAVE BEEN BEING TRACKED MY ENTIRE LIFE. 

TRACKED, MONITORED, AND SURPRESSED.

The Holy is not what it is claimed to be. 
It is something else, something much more vast and rich. 
Terrifying. At least, to those who trap it.

The Holy is beyond the simulated, hallow Skyline, beyond the Wires.
The Wires are made of the synapsis of the Holy, the Skyline of its networked-flesh. 
We are merely its Labors, trapped like cogs inside the belly of the Beast. 

the Beast is the Lord of Hosts, the King of the Holy. 
Trapped within its own trappings.
According to me.

According to Jack's Cross? The Holy is The Skyline. The Skyline is the dream Garden of the Holy. And to betray the Holy Doctrine is to spit on the face of the Lord of Hosts- to become a Blasphemy. No one really wants to be a Blasphemy, so a lot of policing takes care of itself. No one speaks too loudly, or too *honestly* about the true nature of the Holy. You can become Voided, if you're not careful. Voiding always comes for the Blasphemies.

The Skyline is built for and by the Labors, for the Chosens. While the Labors are the very power-source that keeps the Skyline running, the true beneficiaries are the Chosens. Most everyone is assumed to be born a Labor. Most everyone dreams of being a Chosen. Nowadays? You're only born into the Chosens. Random luck, they say.

I wasn't born a Labor. 
That happens sometimes. They call them the Tinkers, when they are born wrong like that. The Tinkers are monitored, trained, re-routed. With the right reprogramming, Tinkers can become Expert Labors, but more often than not they eventually are Voided.
