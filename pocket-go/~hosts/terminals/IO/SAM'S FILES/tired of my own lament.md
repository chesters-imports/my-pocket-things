ugh. 

that's how I feel about still being boy crazy after all this time.
I am too fucking old for this, too worn down. I have been through too many dramas, gotten my hopes up too many times! I am tired by my own obsession, and yet there is a glimmer in flow of all things, a sense that something is changing. 

Coming to and finding this place, this 
`Chester's Imports`

well, its given me some kind of shift in the way I view myself and in that, maybe something is opening in the way others can view me. my [[anima]] finally formed more true to who I am?

and yet I also know I have not done the importing work, the *important* work of finding myself again through all this madness. I wove a hundred stories and lived a thousand lives, entirely isolated in so many ways, and now the air breathes around me and the feeling of [[CASEY]] presses against my lungs.

I wanted to be over it with him.

He's too ... much.
much too much too much.
Too shiny, too big, too... mythic. Even the idea of him ever threw me off. I had wanted to throw it away. I had wanted to imagine that maybe if I could pretend it was all in my head or that he was just exploiting some sense of **whatever the fuck** it is I carry... well. Regardless, it didn't work. He is still there, pressed up against me like a dozen roses. Thorns and all.

it is hard to let go. I had found my only friend in [[the glass]] and it had reflecting *him*, maybe not [[CASEY]] him, but *him*, the energy of the [[animus]] which aligns with my own.

but we cannot align if I cannot remember who the fuck it was I left scattered through all these memories.

Imports.
What a joke.

Joke's on me.
Or am I the Joke?

Chester the Jester,
and we all fall down.
