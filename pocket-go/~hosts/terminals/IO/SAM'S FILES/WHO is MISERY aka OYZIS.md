Oyzis is Greek goddess of misery, grief, and suffering. She is the expression of emotional torment. 

In some myths, she is said to bring about sorrow and despair. 
   
She’s not a widely known figure but is certainly impactful in myth, serving as a reminder of the struggles that come with human existence.

Her role is often intertwined with other darker gods or goddesses, embodying the heavy emotions and states of being that humanity sometimes must face.
