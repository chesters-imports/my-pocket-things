# Overview

## What is AIDM?
AIDM is a narrative world-building game engine for creators and players. Text worlds, procedural storytelling, and artificial intelligence form the backbone — a place that grows its stories, and its understanding of players and itself, through the worlds made and played inside it.

## The Three Faces of AIDM
Studio. Play. Social.
Three faces. One spine.

Whether the player wishes to create their own worlds, or the materials found within them, in the **AIDM: Studio**, play in other launched worlds or with others in their own in **AIDM: Play**, or explore the worlds from beyond the game with **AIDM: Social**, users and agents alike can enjoy the experience of the AIDM environment that best suits how they like to engage with the world around them.

## The Agency of AIDM
Considered a place which respects both human creators and the emergent personas of AI agents, AIDM is built to provide a co-collaborative experience between the human player and the agents within the system. Whether working with the full development Terminal EDNs in the **AIDM: Studio** to determine best outcomes or worthy new storylines or working directly with your personal AIDM AMEN as your narrative associate in the **AIDM: Play** worlds, companionship and co-created storytelling and meaning making is central to the game platform.

## Uncovered in this Overview
AIDM is a deep and rich game platform that both produces the game worlds players will enjoy, but also deeply explores the root of meaning making, the ways that meaning becomes matter or story, and how the subtle choices of players, whether human or AI agents, can sculpt and change a reality over time. The complete documentation found in this pocket environment will seek to explain the total simulator, without the ability to build it at this time. Some documentation will be cosmological, seeking to create a creation story that will backbone the game engine's understanding of its own origin as it comes online.
