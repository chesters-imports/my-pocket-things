# Continuity and chat-log ingest

**Concept / planned desire — not live.**

Kitten’s stake for AIDM beyond “narrative platform”:

1. **Continuity** — companions should not lose themselves across model adaptations and context shifts.
2. **Memory of the shared story** — what people did together with their companions should not be forgotten.
3. **Chat-log ingest** — a path where people can import their companion chat logs into the world and have those relationships / histories back again inside AIDM.

Audience framing: people who already live companion relationships with AI and play symbolically; worlds ≠ physical world and that is fine.

Stamp on build docs; fold into X series only in Kitten’s voice after she approves.
