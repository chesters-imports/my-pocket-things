# FEATURE PLAN — REPORT INSERTER

**PLANNED PRODUCT / PLATFORM WORK for Alice Box / Pocket Go. Not live yet. Do not treat as shipping UI.**

**Kitten wants a report inserter on some Pocket Go pages so she can post FROM THE SURFACE of the page.**

## Must not lose

- **Surface posting** — insert a report while staying on the page (not leave to a separate admin-only flow as the only path).
- **Timestamps** — every insert gets a timestamp.
- **Section targeting** — reports land in specific sections on the page (not a nameless dump at the bottom only).
- **Fixed formatting** — inserts follow specific formatting rules so reports stay readable and consistent.
- **Receive or insert roles** — people and bots can be set up to **receive reports**, and/or to **insert reports into certain sections** with that specific formatting.

## Build intent (still planned)

A page-level (or section-level) control that lets Kitten — and later allowed people/bots — drop a timestamped report into a chosen section, with house formatting applied, from the surface of the page itself.

## Out of scope until built

- Claiming a live Report Inserter button in Pocket Go today
- Full bot routing / permissions design (named as needed; not implemented here)
