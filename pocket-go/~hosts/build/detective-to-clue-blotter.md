# the build plan from sdk (via Buddy)

**Rename / restructure intent:** Detective cabinet → **clue cabinet / clue blotter**. Less metadata shelf. More stickies on the page.

Librarian stays the clean frontmatter / provenance cabinet (catalog card). Lore cards stay shipped fold-notes (often off-page, inbox, classed). Detective was drifting meta and colliding with that shape. Clue blotter is different: **your clue directly on the page**, peelable, dated.

# what a clue sticky is
- Short blotter line: what doesn't fit / what you noticed
- **Dated stamp** of when you thought of this clue (not just when the file was minted)
- Who left it (open: Kitten only for now, or whoever notices — Buddy / Reed work-lane / etc.)
- Optional: points at a gap, a claim, a missing deed (e.g. Crow after-claim vs nothing-after)
- Stays sticky — not a dossier, not Librarian fields

# not the same as lore cards
Lore cards = meaning folds, often classed and shipped. Clue blotter = investigation surface living **on** the page you are looking at, like a detective's pad with time-stamped thoughts.
