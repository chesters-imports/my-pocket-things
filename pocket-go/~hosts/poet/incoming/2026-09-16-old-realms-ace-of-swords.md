# Ace of Swords

This sword was a gift my parents gave me
I received no lessons on 
How to hold my body in its power
How to wield its weighted edges. 
My sword was never delicate, 
slicing flesh on either side
Enough 
Enough to bleed out a new thoughtform
Or to cut another down to size.
My young fool cut on outstretched palms 
This path which leads through
Not wicked but wonderfully wooded
Newness. 
This weapon, cursed at its blade
Is now my stronghold, gripped at the hilt
With a will never afraid to keep grabbing
For Victory. 
Or better yet, 
Peace.
