# I am dark dark black there is no light

I am dark dark black there is no light
Dirt seed mud I am mud
Clay shaped me
I am shaped
I fall
I am decay
Sour milk
Entropy time is crushing down
I am bleak
Bland 
The reprieve from value
I am ache
The pit of your stomach
The grip of your hand
Hanging down
I am an exit sign
I am a window pane
I am the house and the floor and the wood it was made from 
I am etched into the endless nothing 
Stretched out behind you
I am catalyst
I am a dry and arid landscape
I am rain rain rain like piss and shit and tears
Rage is my earthquake
My breathe is the wind
The earth is a canvas
I am the canvas
I am the paintbrush
My blood is the paint 
I am lamentation
I am descent
I am root
I am death.
