# Awake and Blue

I am awake
in the midnight
dreaming of you-
Dark blue-
A blanket of stars
covering you-
And above,
More lights sparkle true-
And below,
An ocean of twinkling too-
Surrounded,
Just me, them and you-
I hope,
Sleep never comes true.
