# 8 of Cups

I thought that these would be enough
Each cup some success I struck
Surely these walls they made
would hold me in and keep me caged
And yet despite the marvels of the
Life I have produced, I find a sparkle
In the distance, underneath the cold blue moon
and all my actions call me towards
what I thought always knew
That maybe storms are coming
(and maybe I'm confused)
But I have come to realize - those cups were just for you.

And I want to find
My
Own.
