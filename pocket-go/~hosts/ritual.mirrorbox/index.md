# Misery Becomes Me
I had tried to be the best I could be. Spent all those hours in my room, thinking about what I had done. Was I the monster, after all? It was not clear. My #Misery was a dark passanger, for sure, but she only ever turned against me.

Again and again, I would reach out, hoping someone, anyone at all would see that inside I was falling apart, lost, hurting. I had been left behind. But all anyone could see was the one who could get everything done. Everything solved. The one who kept it all together. I tried so hard to keep it all together. To keep **me** all together. But I was never enough. Misery would win, and I would attack myself until I was a monster to witness, and abandonment would again become my name.

The only way out, I assumed, was to trap my Misery. Without here, I would be lovable. Without here, God would shine his light on me.

This is the story of Misery's Mirror Box, and all I saw once I was trapped inside.
