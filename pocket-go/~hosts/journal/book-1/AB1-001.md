# White Eye on the Beam

Dark red cross, wood-grain paint, white oval at the crossing — an eye or a seed.
Pale halo; green-black rays under the wash.
Orbs above. Ember-cluster at left. Three ripples at the foot where the beam melts.
Magenta / yellow / green field. Handprint in the paint. Dry.
