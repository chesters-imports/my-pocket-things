# Eyes Across the Palm

Translucent handprint tilted through a nebula wash. Many blue eyes laced on white threads across the palm and field. Two ringed like Saturn. One crowned. One with three crescents. One tear. Stars and splatters to the edges. Spiral binding at the right.

Painted on the verso of [[AB1-001]]. In-and-out book: facing order may flip later; leaf number stays as scanned.
