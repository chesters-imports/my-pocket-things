# Full Moon Interpretation

Answers to the Thoth spread on [[CPJ-033]].

Best Qualities: Zero indicates the intra-personal intersection, a place of internal cohesion. I am home anywhere when I live within my center. I am harmony and naivety.

Unrecognized potential: I am called to make a difference. I have the tools to become my image of The Queen - My skills and weaknesses combine to make me uniquely qualified to achieve my sacred destiny.
