# Monday Means Reality

January 13

[sun]

Monday means reality, work, focus. I want to float into the clouds of all things and not have to try. I want to consider the stars and the colors of light and dream about connections that can be felt but not seen. The reality of reality feels harder to swallow as I dive deeper into my care. I just want to work at the school and try to raise children of light, both mine and the others drawn to the community. I was driven away by her and she left marks on everything, and as I heal the more I feel I tried to create a false truth of me. This reality with its game deadlines and its bosses and those who need to be bossed it just seems utterly disingenuous. This is the dream. Maybe that is why so many stories written say to wake up. But we are deep in sleep, deeply not part of ourselves. We have de-integrated our self so completely from our true nature that it hurts to be alive, our physical bodies suffer - first in the mind, the center, and then slowly expanding out.
