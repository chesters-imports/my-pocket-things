# Illusive Friendship

Jan 9, 2020

[sun glyph]

Realistically, friendship has always been illusive to me. Awkward years compounded with the kind of shyness that comes from feeling completely out of your skin, even further compounded with constant moving communities and schools. Online was a place that never moved but it was not like the online social media of these times. I digress. The point is. I always wanted to belong, be bonded, to have long lasting friends. To me, it seemed important, connections where I bring value always mattered but illuded me.

Anxious, shy, ungrounded, I never connected well. Through the grace of art I found Shane, and through the grace of his mother, all he cared about was his inward world, no need for friends, no want for community, and so this piece of the puzzle that is me remained missing. I think this is a key to why I allowed Her into my life so deeply and without reason. I needed a nanny. I needed a friend, I sought external validation that I had value. With her and her family, I could join my family and create the community!
