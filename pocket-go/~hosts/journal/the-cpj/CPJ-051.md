# Thought of WHY

LIBRA
JAN 16, 2020
[half-filled circle]

Spirit had no mass, no body, absolutely nothing. A baby or even less, hard to put all as nothing into words. It came into being with nothing but its love to give and create, causing the ripple of everything, even to the first civilizations, or even before to formations of gas and chemical compounds to form planets. It was trial and error, it was a cultivation of pure energy performing at it truest level. Eventually, a creature or man is formed with a real body but with little more mind than an animal, one of the everything, safe and connected. But just as a spark creates it all, so does -THOUGHT- begin. And not the thought of hunger, but the thought of PHILOSOPHY, of WHY. For some inexplicit reason, a voice begins inside, a subconscious-conscious mid point. And at this mid point it is unclear what this voice, the voice of -US- is, that we split from it, and suddenly our own fear-- I SHOULDN'T
