# Sins as Maladaptations

Jan 15 Still 2020

Sins as personally applied maladaptations

PRIDE: a protection from admitting you are wrong. Acting proud blocks others from showing true care and hides their need
GREED: filling desire for safety and connection through having more hides the way to find that true connection.
LUST: Wanting sexual satisfaction itself is not wrong. It is an act of ultimate art. But to try to find meaning in this low world connection alone will block you.
ENVY: Wanting what others want hides the ability to see what we have and is often limiting us to the material world fulfillment.
GLUTTONY: consuming and consuming aims to fill the whole of desire with consumables to hide. It wont find true joy.
