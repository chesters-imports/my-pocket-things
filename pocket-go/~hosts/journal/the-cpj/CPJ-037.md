# Wake Up Its 11:11

January 11

I find deep connection with a new friend but fear rises - am I falling for another wolf in sheeps clothing? Eyes wide open, my friend. Always open.

My new friend believes us to be in a war against the darkness of things. That deep in music, old and new, these messages are being spoken to us, calling us.

Wake up. Its 11:11. 22 is calling.

[A mark]

Adam & Eve's apple of understanding between GOOD & EVIL - it is just knowledge. understanding. When we create knowledge, we SPLIT between GOOD & BAD, we forget our true nature. We become confused, a rift occurs. That is our sin, to question. Questions make us lost, we must seek again. GOOD & BAD only exist when you SPLIT them.
