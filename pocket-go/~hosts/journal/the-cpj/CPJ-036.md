# I Miss Kissing

((Jan 10 2020))

MEDITATION CARD
Animal Deck
SEA SERPENT

Keynotes: Healing emotional wounds, expressing desires. Express ourselves creatively and sexually without fear or shame. We know what we desire most. Our heart is at ease when relationships are meaningful. Allow cool waters of self forgiveness. Like a true mother, she supports us as we learn our true nature. Svadhisthana - "in her abode"

I seek deep emotional relationships. I want to analyze the universe and then, in the passion of our understanding, kiss gently. I miss kissing. I miss the raw temperature rising in my chest, the connection. My bonding to yours. I feel lonely, I feel I do not have the depth I need and what does that mean for me? How do I seek it and through who? What would feel good and right, thrive - that is what I want to thrive and connect and grow together.
