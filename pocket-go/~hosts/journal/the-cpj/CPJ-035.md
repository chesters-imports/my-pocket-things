# Your Sun in My Moon

JAN 10 2020

[face in circle]

I went so far for you, whoever you was
Sought to fill the blank spaces in me.
Was it so wrong? I knew I needed Other.
But what does it mean? Other, other what?
Other you, or yous, or was it only me
who I needed to see?

"I keep digging myself down deeper"
but I stop now, I will never get
where you are
We are always together
I, a reflection of anything before me,
the universe observing itself.

So now I go so far for me
to reflect You in your glory,
Your Sun in my moon
Rotated, rotating
Sometimes hidden and sometimes,
Like tonight
Full -
Found -
Shining.

Are you the reflection I am looking
to see?
