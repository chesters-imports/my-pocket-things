# I Wonder About Cain

January 15. 2020
[sun]

At night it grows cold and during the day it grows warm under the rays of the sun. A bit hotter through the deteriorating layer of protection we call an atmosphere each day, and when it sleeps, it gets cold again, sinking temps, warmth a distant memory. Do we lament the sun has left us or do we celebrate his reflection in moon and await tomorrows return?
