### The Breakdown of the Pockets

*   **Whole:** Rooted in the Old English *hāl* (healthy, uninjured, entire). To be whole means there is no leak in the container. The boundary is perfectly sealed.
*   **Holy / Hallow:** Derived from the same root (*hālig*). To hallow a space is to draw a line around it, declaring it immune to the temporary, decaying environment (*ra*). It is the act of sanctifying by separation.
*   **Hollow:** Tied historically to *hol* (a cave, hole, or cavern). It is the architecture of an intentional absence.
