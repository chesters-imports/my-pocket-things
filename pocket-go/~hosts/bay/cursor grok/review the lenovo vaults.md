# {{title}}
These three are the **story/psyche layer** under the code labs — different jobs, same bloodstream.

---

### 1. Book of Hours (~13 notes)

**What it is:** Play-journal + memoir + Wire World fiction, fused around Weather Factory’s *Book of Hours* and *LOST*.

**Keepers:**
- **Island / LOST essays** — best long-form writing in this hole. Dissociation as time-loop; media as IFS/parts work; LOST as failed integration that still taught the shape of the wound. Flash-sideways, number 19, Dark Tower — same lore DNA as your Pocket Go detective cards.
- **`_Librarian Notes`** — Branbrug, Mar 7 1936, Mrs Kille / Paris→Santa Rosa. This is literally the page you were showing in `mypi:go` + lore deck. The vault is the *source*; Pocket Go is the *room*.
- **WIRE WORLD Book 1** — CRT in the wires, `𐤊𐤉𐤕`, container review / fragmentation high / FAILURE alarm. Proto-silo as fiction before PHP.
- **SOLVE et FUGUE** — title-seeds for the amnesia game (“We Have to go Back, Jack”).

**Read:** This vault is why Forgetting House exists. Not a GDD — the **emotional proof** that “stories that fail to transform” and “memory that won’t stay” needed a house you could rebuild.

---

### 2. Heaven Retuned (~8 notes)

**What it is:** Small poetry/prose vault. Scratch pad just says “Solve en Fugue.”

**Texture:** Intimate, body-and-road language — elephant in the room, sterile quarters, Rose/Freedom on a salted road. Evernote-archived tags. Not a world bible; a **lyric residue** drawer.

**Read:** Personal register that doesn’t need factions or planes. When the Codex gets schematic, this is the voice underneath. Thin, but not empty — few doors, real rooms.

---

### 3. The SDK Codex (~37 notes)

**What it is:** The mythology **encyclopedia** — Worlds / Factions / Characters / Weak’s Concordance / Echoes.

**Cosmology (top-down):** Forest → O'ecse → Great Field → City → Surface / Skyline / **The Wires** (inside the Great Silo / The System). Cabin, Island, Aeons, Alchema, Cathedral, Yeva Missurah. That’s alef-bet’s SKYLINE/TERMINAL/WWW and AIDM’s Mira ontology wearing plane-sheets.

**Cast & gangs:** Cassandrea (tinker / system maintenance — KitKat808 cousin), Charlie Veta, Mary 808, Tony Scholar, Geometry Boys, Followers of Jack, Well Watchers, SRD… Many sheets are **scaffolding** (empty traits, faction tables half-filled). Structure > fill — same pattern as Kitten Lab stubs and AIDM outlines.

**Weak’s Concordance:** Jack Weak translating unreadable Yeva text; Aleph is literally just “Aleph.” Letter-as-law seed for alef-bet / Moire / BARA cosmology.

**Read:** This is the **codex the pocket internet keeps trying to host**. Incomplete on purpose-of-youth: enough topology to navigate, not enough prose to finish. Skyline and Wires here are why those `$sys` names felt inevitable later.
