# intake

This is the loading dock. Conversations land here - yours, an agent's - parked on the floor until they move into a real room or die on the pallet.

The striped door is the cue. boybots, cursor grok, and gemini are slips on the corrugated wall. Open a dump and read it on the crate under the lamp. Nothing here has to be filed yet.
