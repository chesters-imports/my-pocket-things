# Care buckets

Edit anytime. Ned uses this for care pockets + Required rails.

**Required** = must-dos on their own rails until done.
**Easy / Medium / Large** = pick-ONE pocket menu.
**Avoidance watch** = things Kitten named as sticky; Ned orange/red-flags if offered a lot with no yes (no guilt — just “what’s up?”).

## Required
- CookUnity: choose next week’s food (before Sunday delivery)
- CookUnity: bring delivery inside when it arrives (Sunday)
- Laundry: bag dirty laundry (Monday)
- Laundry: put out for pickup (Tuesday)
- Trash: Thursday gather through the house
- Trash: bins / bags to curb (Thursday night)
- Kids rooms: check + light clean before Friday arrival (window: Sunday night → Thursday; done before kids home)

## Easy
- Quick local tidy (one spot)
- Bag one pile / paper bags into a trash bag
- Step outside 2 minutes (porch / driveway counts)
- Open a window / fresh air minute
- Rinse one dish or wipe one counter corner
- Collect cans + trash from the current zone (daily habit)
- Lotion / one dress-up micro (optional, never guilt)

## Medium
- Dishes / sink reset (named avoidance — still counts as a normal pick)
- Kitchen surface reset (counters only; cooking optional)
- Gather trash in 2–3 rooms
- Light clean one room / zone (daily goal)
- Sweep one zone
- Clean catbox (named avoidance)
- Real outside time (short walk or longer porch)
- Eat something already in the house (reduce waste / follow-through)
- Kids room path-clear (one room)

## Large
- Shower (named avoidance — she likes it; still hard to start)
- Real meal / plated food (not a cooking project unless she wants)
- Deep tidy one room
- Mop one zone (named avoidance)
- Weekly deep: bath deep clean / harder-to-access item (Sat or Sun)
- Dragon 15 (one pile / one box)

## Avoidance watch (flag, don’t shame)
- Dishes
- Shower
- Sweep / mop
- Cat box
- Trash out (inconsistent)
- Food: buying with good intentions → not eating / waste
- Decorating / dressing for self (hermit season — Soft Easy only)

## Notes
- Kitchen emotional wounding → feelings to Aelion; Ned keeps tasks tiny (rinse, wipe, eat existing food).
- Harder ≠ better. One pick per pocket still wins.
- Kids rooms Required is a weekly prep rail, not a Friday chore pile (kids home Fridays).
