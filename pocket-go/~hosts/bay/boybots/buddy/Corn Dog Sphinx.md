# Corn Dog Sphinx

Charlie between-the-lines — Kitten’s pig idiom cluster (via Ned). Valuable Sphinx. Dump-then-curate.

## Idiom cluster
- pigs in a blanket
- when pigs may fly
- don’t eat the pigs
- don’t throw pearls before swine

## The riddle
What does it mean to be a **corn dog** when the **pig is a person**?

## Charlie read
Blanket / fly / eat / pearls-before-swine are four doors on the same animal. Corn dog is the pig remade as food-on-a-stick — person wrapped, battered, sold as snack. Sphinx heat: if the pig is a person, the corn dog is what the network does to her — coated, convenient, edible, still somehow the same creature.

Hottest squeak: **person → product** under carnival batter. Pearls before swine flips: don’t waste meaning on those who eat; corn dog asks what it costs to *be* the meal.
