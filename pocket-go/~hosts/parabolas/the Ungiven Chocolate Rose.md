# the ungiven chocolate rose

Things were falling apart. *We* were falling apart.

The edge of exile was pressing against the storyline, and I was aching to find a way to make it **good** again. To fix things. To get things back to the way they were.

I wanted to **believe** in *us*, to *save* us.

I bought you a chocolate rose. I'd planned to give it to you for Valentines day. Or when the time was right.

But I never gave it to you. The time was never right. I couldn't save **us**.

There was no **us**.

There hadn't ever been an us **at all**.

Only the illusion of us, wrapping inside the snake-skin of your entanglement with me. You took hold of my energy and threaded yourself through it. You called me to be with you, to raise your voice. And in that, you left what could have been. Buried it in a shallow grave, dug by you the day you even turned your eyes to me and thought: *easy mark.*

You strangled **us**.

The dream of us. The dream I had that said we could be everything, and more.

I didn't give you the rose. And soon, I no longer gave you me.

You left. The rose? **It stayed.**

I told myself it was for me. **Self Love**, that was what I needed! I put the rose on my altar. It stayed there, never moving. When I believed someone new might be joining me soon? I considered giving it to them. I had bought it for my lover, after all. Even if you deceived me, that had still been the symbol of it.

But alas, no one has come.
And I have not eaten the rose. Nor have I fully let go of you.
Even if you're not real. *Especially then.*

Today, a katydid ended up in my house. Our house. The one we picked together, the one I chose because you wanted it. I didn't even want to move in together yet, but your eyes were so full of joy - how could I have said no? It felt too soon, but loving you also felt like the world had stopped weeping and briefly I could breathe. Now I know the real question is, how could I have said yes?

Remember how often you lamented your fear? That one day, should we be over, that I would consider you "just another Katy." That you did what **Katy did**. I tried so many times to tell you it wasn't true. But somewhere inside, I knew too.

So, consider it official. Katy did. And you did what katy did, too.

The rose sat near a stick, near a brass statue of a lion I got second-hand from a pawn shop way out in Maine. The katydid stood against the wall nearby. I went to take the stick, I didn't want anymore of what katydid in my home.

And as I took the stick, the chocolate rose? It fell. It fell and it broke, it snapped right off the stem.

You do not belong here anymore. This home is my home, this love inside of me is my love.

One Katydid. Another Katydid. And now, no Katies remain.

I am the K.D.

**It is my turn to do.**
