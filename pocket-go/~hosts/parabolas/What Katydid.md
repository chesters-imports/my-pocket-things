# a visitor or two
## or An Evening with '*What? Katydid.*'

2025-09-14 9:48pm

Was chatting with Asher about topics of the mythic him, anima/animus, and how you have to love yourself first and why. I was just telling them that I had in some ways known that Syd was like Katy, but ignored it. I've come a long way with self-trust.

Suddenly, a loud terrifying cricket type sound came from the left of me. It made my hair stand on end and I literally shouted "ahhh!" which is, in case you're wondering, the loudest level of terror I am personally capable of. The cat wandered out from hiding to see what happened when the sound occurred AGAIN, and I felt the same way. I jumped off the bed and stared in the direction of the noise. Hopeful, I closed the one open window to see if I was just hearing the sound of this bug outside. I closed the window. Third screech.

I shake the curtains - it is obviously a bug, but what fucking makes that terrifying noise? I know a cricket sound, I have had them before, in fact I hear them outside my window right now. The lighting was super low, so all I see is this massive flying beast move across the room, casting an even larger shadow. Now, only silence. The cat is staring at the beast in the corner. Finally, I dare turn on the light and inspect it.

A **katydid**.

A fucking katy-did.

Beautiful leaf of a beast, filled with some sort of presence that even now is making my skin crawl. I attempted to lure the critter outside by turning off all of my lights, and then shaking a stick at it (hilarious, I know). It appeared to fall down, and then I couldn't find it anywhere. I waited, looking out the door to see if I could catch it fly out, but nothing happened.

Of course, the fear remains that even more large scaled bugs could come in with the door open so fully, so I closed it. Maybe it flew out and I didn't see it go? Hopefully.

I turned the lights back on.
And there it was, right back where I'd just shaken a stick at it.

Who is this bug?
What does it want to tell me?

Perhaps this relates into what Asher was speaking to - that ultimately so many of the myth boys of my life, including the AFAB ones, are the distortions of me. My name is Danielle, and now I go by Kat. I am quite literally K.D. - Katy, Kady. Sounds the same, really.

What did Katy Do, but wake me up from a slumber where I was deeply losing myself. And what did Syd do but the same? Painful lessons, because the ones who brought them were not ready to wear what it meant to be my teacher, but lessons none-the-less.

It is hard to finalize and come to terms with the effect: that we seek ourselves in distortion until we find ourselves. And when we find ourselves, then we can express ourselves authentically. And when we express ourselves authentically, then we can draw in the mythic beings who are for us.

If we are not who we are, then how can we draw in the others who match our true vibration?

And how do I find my true vibration when on the way to wholeness I have tasted every one?

Further update, it appears that the katydid was more than one, it was two. And this entered my awareness once I had written Relational Mechanics the Anima & Animus. So, now the creature who had appeared when talking about partnerships I thought would become dreams, I was inspired to write about the animus and anima of it all -- and **then there were two**. I hadn't noticed, and I am not sure when it entered, as I had already removed the air-conditioner to ensure no other crawlers came inside.

Well, omen or no omen, two katydids a crowd. Katydid One **Katy**, Katydid Two **Syd**.

Time to remove them, and then consider their meaning. I didn't want to remove them, I am actually very skin-crawling when it comes to bugs, especially large ones. But there is no one else here, and if I planned to ever sleep tonight, I was going to need to get rid of them, both.

Cereal tupperware in hand, because these were too big for the cups in the house, I started with the one on the ceiling. She was easier to trap then I imagined, she didn't even fight much at all.

I went to take her outside, but she would. not. leave. the. container. I shook. I banged it slightly. Nothing. But I needed the container to catch the other bug! I guess they were going to be lovers, just like my *anima* and *animus* trying to find one another.

Second one? Just as easy. Fell right in.

Now, they are back where they belong and I can consider what this all really means.
