# {{title}}
The #greek #goddess of misery, [[Erysichthon]]. A concept, a force of the felt-sense of misery. Oyzis (or Oizys) is indeed a Greek goddess of #misery, #grief, and #suffering. She represents the #darker-aspects of #emotional-torment.

In some myths, she is said to bring about #sorrow and #despair. Though not a known figure compared to gods like [[Zeus]] or [[Hades]], her stories speak to the struggles we all experience, both those #human and those #divine.

Her role is often intertwined with other #dark-gods or goddesses, her stories fueling the heavy emotions and states of being that humanity sometimes must face.
