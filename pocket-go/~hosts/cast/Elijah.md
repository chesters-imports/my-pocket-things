### The Prophet of [[Yeva Canon]]

#Elijah was a #prophet and #miracle worker who lived in the #northern kingdom of #Israel during the reign of King Ahab, according to the Books of Kings in the Hebrew Bible. In 1 Kings 18, Elijah defended the worship of the Hebrew deity #Yahweh over that of the #Canaanite deity #Baal. God also performed many miracles through Elijah, including resurrection, bringing fire down from the sky, and ascending to heaven alive. 

He is also portrayed as leading a school of prophets known as "[[the sons of the prophets]]"
