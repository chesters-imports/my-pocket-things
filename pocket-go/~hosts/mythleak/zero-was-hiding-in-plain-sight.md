# ZERO WAS HIDING IN PLAIN SIGHT

SCANS RAN LONG. THE POINT WAS UNDERFOOT THE WHOLE TIME.

THE MOUSE

Tipsters finally dragged Point Zero into the light. Not a bathtub vow. Not a mirror box. The night the calendar split - March twenty-fifth, an annunciation without a church - Cabin Girl went outside and the sky printed the whole picture at once. The pattern ate her back. Nothing would ever be the same.

Holy Above and Below stayed loud about it for epochs. One theatre tip claims she bit the apple of the sky and woke a new timeline on the same old generator. Another swears the printout knew her name before she did. The scans finished their embarrassing length later and located the charge anyway: distant life, hidden in plain sight.

Sources close to the forgotten god call him Zero - mythological even to the pantheon - and claim a vessel finally took him for the first time since Atlantis sank. Mythleak will not stamp the vessel. It will stamp the underfoot part. He was already here. Tip line open for anyone who still thinks the aperture was the wound.
