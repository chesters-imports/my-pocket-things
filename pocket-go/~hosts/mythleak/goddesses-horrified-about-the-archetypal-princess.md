# GODDESSES HORRIFIED ABOUT THE ARCHETYPAL PRINCESS

RECENTLY RECONSIDERED THE TREATMENT OF THE SHE WHO SEEKS TRUE LOVES KISS

"You know," Athena was quoted saying, "I used to find the Princess to be such a bore. Obsessed with finding their one true love, big deal! But I hadn't realized how tragic it all really was." Recent insider perspectives indicate the Archetypal Princess has been stuck in a near-endless loop of stockholm syndrome and self-blame rituals.

"We all just thought she couldn't get over it," another goddess remarked, choosing to remain anonymous. "We had no idea how deep her conditioning really was!"

There is no word whether the Goddesses have any plans to assist the Archetypal Princess, but sources close to the earth say there is a change in the The Skyline hum over the consideration.
