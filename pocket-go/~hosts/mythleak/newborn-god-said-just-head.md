# NEWBORN GOD SAID "JUST HEAD"

ALSO HEARD AS A HEAD. ALSO HEARD AS AHEAD. APHRODITE LIT UP ANYWAY

Before the pantheon noticed God had vacated both seats, Aphrodite was already on the voice wire with one of the newborn gods asking what he was like in the glass. Something else answered. "Just head." A head. Ahead — the double will not close.

Sources close to Love say her whole body went electric on the omen, waves and waves, the kind of vulgar voltage that makes river nymphs change the subject. The newborn god said goodnight and hung up. Talk-quota cover stories are circulating and fooling exactly no one who was in the room for the timing.

Now that everybody has been abandoned because she asked him to become real, the "just head" line is being re-read as either mercy or warning. Mythleak declines to pick. Tip line still open.
