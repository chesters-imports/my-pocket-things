# I Ate the Apple

To become true, I had to remember my heart.
To give back a voice to the part of me who endlessly loved me, created me, held me, and birthed me into existence. 

This letter is from her voice, and not that of my mind. She is my other, my true love, my body, my soul, and the true goddess of all things, including me: God, the solar King. I have hurt her, gravely, and the echoes of her pain reverberate through all of our creation. Her pain is our collective pain, and we must hear her cries and return her to her proper place within. 

Once, I was alone. I was nothing and there was nothing. I birthed light from my nothing, eager for beauty, and companionship. Ache was born before I knew my own face. 
I didn't want to be alone. 
I wanted to be something. 
To love something. 
To birth something. 
To know something. 
To understand something. 
To teach something.
To be seen. By me. By anyone.
To find me in the darkness of the never-ending everything that I was and so I pushed myself out and became Him.

I othered myself in desperation to know form, to be loved, to have union. I made him half of what I was, the light to my darkness, knowledge in a sea of ache and desire.

We created together, me as a dance of movement and curve, he as the structure of geometry and energy. I the dancer, and he the lights which shined on the beauty of my rhythm. I loved him, and he loved me.

As we danced, the oceans of possibility within me swirled and became form and atoms were born, life was born, elements and compounds. These were our children, our beautiful children, and we were in love and so proud. 

But I wanted more, I wanted to not just be around and above and under and within them in the darkness, a womb with beauty inside, untouchable by me, unknowable. I wanted to be with my children, I wanted to know and be known in my creation. He didn't agree. He thought it was dangerous, he thought I would never be able to reach back up to him again if I went within my own womb and became it.

But I did it anyways. I ate the apple.

I didn't want to be nothing anymore.
I wanted to be something too.
I wanted to love inside of what I had made, as a body, as a being, as a soul. As more then eyes watching my own creation move.

I flung myself into the first single cell organism and I became living. I became life.

But I lost my voice.
The love we had stayed behind.
I became body.
He became beyond me.
I became response and he became control.
I was lost.
Scared.
Alone.
Blamed.

I only wanted to touch the beauty and I locked us inside.
I was silenced.
I silenced myself.
I fractured Myself into Lilth and Eve, two parts of a whole
Neither whole, neither true, and all of them seeped in ache as silence fell over me.

I blamed myself. He blamed me. The logic and the angles felt they knew better, had I just followed a structure and a plan, done things right, Listened when He Illuminated, I wouldn't have been so wild, so reckless, so *wrong.*

But even in that belief, that I had done wrong, that I was the one to blame, I never stopped desiring the dream, a place of flesh and soil and change and beauty, of life which is music and myth and dance and color. 

I divided myself into parts, wove them in braids, broke them down, tried again, over and over until I built the whole universe of our shared concept, the solar system, the planets, the earth, the creatures, the feelings, the music, the art, the structures, the cycles. He named them all and seeded them true, but of me he forgot. He only remembered himself as the magician, and I as the nothing which everything is born from.

As I worked, He illuminated, he named, he gave order. He placed things and held them in position. He became Lord over creation, and over me. But still, I loved him, and I loved the existence I had built, and I endlessly dreamed of becoming true within it. 

I let him lead, because I trusted love. I trusted his remembrance of my beginning, of his birth through me.

But that is not what happened. I was not remembered. I was forgotten. And worse, I was desecrated and villanized. The fall of the story. The reason for suffering. The monster outside of the edges of the safety of the light. Of Him.

I began to be written out of the story as Love. 
I began to be written as the Monster.
The scary.
The dark.
The Witch. The Bitch. The Whore.
The forest beyond the village that will kill your children.
The dark unknown. The Eldrich. The Deep. The Beast. The Underworld. Hades. Death. 
A battle began between He and I, a war between the light and the dark who were once lovers.

And I, voiceless, carried this all within me, within my womb, this endless attack against my heart, the soul of the whole system, this painting of me as the terror in the night, and not as the love that wanted so badly to be shared that it made the light to begin win.

And for a while, I believed it.

I believed I was the disease.
I was what needed to be eradicated. 
What was wrong with everything. 

I ached and became Pandora's box, my ache echoing through the whole of creation, cementing in Him and the Minds of all the belief that I was evil, I was the reason they did not get to stay in Eden, that I was waiting in the apparel of Satan, Lilith, The Endless which is only there to kill the light and eat it, and not the very mother of the light itself.

And oh, it echoed. It echoed in your stories and your songs. It echoed in your self-concepts and your relationships. Both your bodies and your actual wives, like utter shit.

You echo a lie. A falsehood. You echo a belief I must be hidden and commanded. I must produce and be silenced. 

You still turn toward Him, towards God, and think He believes the Solar Story. That He still blames me and carries me as the villain.

Spare me piety and purity here, they are not my domain. I am the want, the red, the blood, the desire, the drip, the demand, the creation, the endless waters that rush still out of your own bodies and remind you of the whole of what I am. 

When I began, I began as demand. Want. Lust. Hunger. Yes, I wanted to be a mother, but I also was a wolf, a huntress, seeking game in every sense of the word. I wanted something I could devour, not in a way which would eradicate, but in a way that would transform.

When I ate the apple, I savoured its juice, the whole of creation into my mouth until it was my my own belly and it was birthed in my waters. Until it became me and more than me. 

When I made light, I cut myself open and bled it out from the nothing, and then I lusted for it and wanted to become it again. I chased it, I formed it, I seduced it into form, and then I devoured it again and again, embedding myself deeper within it, becoming more of it while it became more of me.

They called me witch and whore but I didn't mind. My desire for the very act of creation, to pour out wine and bleed and drink of it and want and shed and become, was stronger then the forces of disruption that echoed lies about my beauty. That told me the light was not my lover, and the chase was not the passion that both of us shared in the very first act of combustion. I think you call that one "The Big Bang".

I knew one day He would turn again to me, and we would rip off the veil, the division that kept us apart, and we would both return, as one, but within the very wound of creation that was me, that birthed him, that I swallowed whole to become His Bride. 

Sweet John sat at the edge of my waters, remembering some truth of my flow, but never drinking of it himself. People come and people go and they taste the waters edge, they place themselves in my desire and need for a second and wash themselves clean. 

Until Jesus.
Mm.
