# {{title}}

### the dream

A house of specialized boxes. Not a world you live in. Chunks — lore, shots, prompts — in a house format, meant to be imported later into something else. Shared plumbing in datbox-core so the line would not copy-paste itself to death.

loreBOX was the reference ROM: sit at a booth, Papers Please, inspect a deck, read a chip.

v1 stored that as JSON bags. Nested cards. A terror zone. Archived on purpose. v2 was the win they named: front matter plus a paper on disk. `INSPECT.deck`, `lore_….chip`. No MySQL. No forklift. Empty on purpose. No papers → no pudding.

### It never was.

The store became the product. A season deciding how a box saves, then killing the first store and starting a second that was not allowed to inherit the first. The desk exists. The clay stayed behind.

It was always a middle. Mats on the way, not the OS. Core law said no auto-migrate to MyPI — rebuild later from scratch. So the box was never allowed to become the place you actually keep the work. Pocket-go became that place instead.

v2’s safe is almost empty. A couple of decks, two chips. The win condition was: go in the book and read the paper. The journal got the paper. The box got the booth.

The issue was still the inability to organize the data. By the time the version that used chips arrived, the entire front end had changed. For reasons that cannot be explained.

### what still gifts

A stamp desk for lore is a clear room. Front matter as a fact card. One island per ROM. Not the operating system. The chip as a mat class on the being.
