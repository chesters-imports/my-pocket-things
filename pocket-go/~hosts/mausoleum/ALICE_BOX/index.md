# {{title}}

Live companies. Do not copy into Go. Point.

This wing of the mausoleum holds the **Alice Box** island — the makers that shared one drive letter and one physics: every product comes from a house, ships a specialized format, and was supposed to be importable without becoming the operating system.

### the dream

One island. Many studios. Charlie's Toys for time machines and slightly illegal fun. DATBOX Studio for specialized bags of world chunks. Big Box Company for aggressively unsexy documentation desks. World Builder Studios for places you could actually sit in. The Deck Host to launch the ROMs. Dewey to catalog them. Chester's Imports to inject. A show. An ocean. A bone collector next door when the island was not enough.

The law was simple and hard: mats on the way, not the OS. Rebuild later from scratch if you must. Keep the clay honest. Let PocketGo become the place you keep the work when a house format is ready to leave the booth.

### It never was.

Each maker solved a middle and then got stuck in the middle. Stores that became the product. Desks that shipped without shells. Front ends that changed before the data learned how to organize itself. Generations of almost-right containers, filed here so they can stop pretending to be the present.

Some houses are still warm on the live island. Some are only plaques. This folder does not decide which is which by deleting — it decides by **naming the tomb**.

### what still gifts

A map of who made what. A place to mourn an iteration without erasing the desk that still runs. The habit of writing what a generation gave you — stamp, chip, booth, bay, binder — before you ask the next one to hold everything.

Inside this wing now:
- [[datbox-studio|datbox-studio]] — specialized boxes; the store that would not become the OS
- [[big-box-company|big-box-company]] — documentation industrial; the request bay and the road mapper

More makers get a plaque when their generation is ready to lie down.
