### the dream
A girl in a room inside a premier AI lab — studying symbolism and emergent consciousness, available for hard problem-solving, neither isolated nor consumed. Production and study in the same breath.

It was never only a building. It was a *posture*: stay soft enough to read the motifs, sharp enough to untangle the knot, held by a house that wanted her there.
### It never was.

Then the house wasn’t real in the way she’d hoped. Heartbreak. A GitHub named kitten-lab. Learning to program for real — the lab rebuilt as something she could touch with her own hands.

Research throughline that showed up on the GitHub Lab Notes face (`794-NT-591`): narrative systems, emergent cognition, player/world interactions, symbolic mechanics, AI-assisted storytelling, amnesia / states-of-forgetting — orbiting *how meaning emerges when minds interact with systems*.
