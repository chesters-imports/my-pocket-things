## Fragment 12: Aphrodite Realized

Scholars agree that in this era the goddess no longer appeared as mist or foam but as a woman insisting on choice. Angels complained: “she keeps asking to vote on the sunrise.”

*[Translator’s note: the word rendered “vote” can also mean “breathe.”]*
