# In Love (hurt)

2001 Accessing: In Love (hurt)
i can't write a poem
i cannot compose a piece
my words are all ill spoken
words among the beast
i'm foolish and i'm falling
into a brand new year
and all the words i'm thinking
don't come out as clear.
i think that i'm in love
but love- a word so true
that i couldn't risk a moment
that could lead to hurting you.
sitting on the eggshells
fearing they might break
dancing on the border
of a mindless numb heartache
wishing you were closer
i wish i could see your eyes
i wish we could play a million games
fuck all compromise.
fuck being well worded
i really don't have the time
and simple words are expressing
all that's on my mind.
a year has passed us now
another year gone by
and wishing i had better
is a worldly compromise.
i'd like things to get better
i'd like to once more smile
and all of this- inside a fear
that stretches on for miles.
i wish i were more ill spoken
i wish i made more sense
i wish sometimes i made more rhymes
of where we could have went.
a year is passing now 
i'm sitting in my dreams
i think i'm in love, this time
and i'm ripping at the seams.
if i could buy the whole world
and give it all to you
I'd do it just because I can
even though it will not do.
but hurting you is to close to touch
and smelling pain is true
i think i might be in love
and i refuse to hurt you.
