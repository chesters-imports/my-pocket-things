# It Hurts

2000 
I can tell you, this pain -- **it hurts.**This is to calm the sense, settle the chamber of rattling in my mind, the pull of my muscles as they ache out for something more. This is to refrain from the power to feel pain, to corrupt and destroy the pieces of me that still remain young, unflawed - they are fragile to the touch of your hand.
This is to die for.
This is the life we've seen on TV, the dramatic encore, more .. more they all yell, more of the pain, for the pain COMFORTS us. The pain replenishes us, reminds us of what we have, for we are making it strong.. But what about the actual feelers of the pain, the anguish, the need to break the shell? This is my walking hell, and this is only here to calm it. To calm it and give it to you, take it or leave it, digest it or bring it back up and throw it away in the gods of all gods, the nothingness that has become my wicked sanity. You do not see me anymore. You are blind to see me for you resist the pain that I may bring, the disgust I may leave settled in your stomach.
But, it's not that bad, right? It's everyone else's pain, each face you watch wander down the street, into the road. Each death, another statistic.. Are you watching it all change now? Each story, each little word.. God, the little words.. The little words that send me screaming, an outburst of what has yet to grow of me. I am still the weak child, your drama only makes me a better actress, for I act for the all of you.. I act with a mind expanded, but I sleep sucking my thumb.. I cry for hours, because I hurt inside. The strong you see is only the weakness' way of protecting me, the wall that seems forever to the next turn.
You can't be inside me. Unless, I let you. You touch me, and I take you inside. I spill to you in words my hate, and you try to make it better.. And that is my decay, for as a person, I addicted myself to the pain that boils in my veins, I have become a user of the stress-less drug.. With pain, you don't have to worry if tomorrow you'll hurt. You figure you will.. and on the good days, you replenish and memorize. You become a lover of the small things in life. And what is wrong with that?
You are inside me, I have let you in. You love me, and my happiness has become the world to you.. (this is to calm the sense, this is to cool the flames..)
But, you are still blind to me, blind to my ways that I have come to hate. You saw a reflection on the wall that was twisted, and so you read it wrong.. and now you do not think I want you anymore. You want to walk away from the treasure I fear..? but as friends, you are still due to run. You do not see me. I am an illusion. I am nothing. The things around us are nothing. We live in nothing, and endlessly seek nothing, and blindly, you know not that it is only nothing that we seek. We are of no importance, and endless dance, a sickened dream. Forever around and around again. How could you flex to the pain? 
This is to calm the nerve.
