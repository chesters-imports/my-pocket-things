# Stupid Girl

2000
we fear these delicate touches.

I'm just a stupid girl.

My means of existence are tainted by a life I don't think I could have really ever led. This are but moments hung in the halls, doorways we never opened. I can cry as many tears as I often think I should -- just to heal you. Just to bring the light back to where it is deserved. God only knows I drift away within a pillow of hate and illusions far beyond their simple understanding, but where does that put me? If they can not understand my words, then why bother to speak them? Perhaps you listen well, perhaps you cannot hear, but these words are my passion. They are the very meaning of my existence, to pull back the skin and reveal the flesh that bleeds inside because of all this.

I live within my pain. It is saturation when I thought there was no color, and yet I'm bold to believe the dawn is changing -- it fuels me for it is my life. Can you see me in the darkness, when I am not there and cannot breathe for my lungs can't take to strain? Will you catch me when I fall and all the darkness shatters around me? It's like dying to the moonlight, soft and yet I'm so alone within a tiny world that I cannot escape. This is my hell, and heaven, for although I'm pulled apart, I am together in a saner place where I can understand the way I think, and how they move so precisely as if they were pieces on a board, this game of life. 

Now please don't get me wrong, it's not my call to make, what's black what's white, or if you really even think what hurts so bad inside is not going to die -- it cannot die. The little things they matter, even if you hide from all of this -- the facade is not a shield, to pretend you are alive and that it's really just okay. This is a world we only feel once, I want to understand. Why lack thought when it brings me feeling, and although to feel may hurt, I refuse to miss the blessed moments when it feels like my heart could pull out of my chest and sing to you. I'm flavor in my words and speech, to feel the inside drawing out. The pencil turns, no strings are pulled -- we are sane within this.

But I am such a stupid girl, with stupid means for tearing out of a past that I never lived. Such a stupid girl with stupid dreams to live a life that people only get when they will fuck and fuck and fuck to get to where they want -- and I'm not easily amused by the flattering feeling that someone wants to fuck my brains out. I'd rather be elegance in disposition, where means of conduct may not be rich, but God at least they are true.

I want to feel my way to where I stand upon a mountain, and I can't ever stop screaming. The vibrations feel like morning glory, bitter happiness where it was all rain. You may not love the sun, but i sure do -- when it rises above the pain and makes the land look new again. The flowers dance in praise, for I am not alone.
