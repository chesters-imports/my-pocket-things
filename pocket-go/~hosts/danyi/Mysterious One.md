# Mysterious One

• Mysterious One •

Shares these lies with one another
The lights to dark to get away.
No more love to grant one thing
No one love to be led astray.
Hidden darkness melts the soul
Tales of death, left untold
Cast away your dark good-byes
Let me roam or let me die.
If you play with fire
You may already burn
The mysteries get deep
When you need what you earn.
