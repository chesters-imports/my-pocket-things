# So Blind

2000 Delicate spell caster, I wish I weren't so blind.

I wish I didn't come to fool myself into believing these gentle facades, the blade slices only at your dreams, and the tears are promises -- that you haven't yet died inside. Is my health in knowing I can produce change? That my meager existence is not as childish as it seems? You walk in the door and leave me everything I ever wanted, and then you walk right out as if to never say goodbye? It's strictly my illusions, held tight by this suspended hope that I will induce creation, and become true to myself, and God. I don't care anymore if that creator exists, I only wish to feel as though there is a point to all this. Walls crumble and destruction is received in the bitter awareness that this could all be for nothing.
Coax the shell away, it'd seem to simple to do. To wash away a dream, a nightmare rather, that drifts into my fears. My blood is but the same tears you cry, my screams are what curl your skin at night. Don't you see I think of you? Don't you see what you've done to me? Shattered the glass, and now I'm left open, and you are gone.

A silly game. A trick facade. To see past these moments, what a silly little thing to shatter it all. The phone never rings, the moves are never made. Where is tomorrow from here? It's bound by the wrists and waiting for me to stop living in the past -- but I've come to only wish the present was now, and the past was going to happen once more.

Tainted are these division bells, splitting you and I.
I'll never forgive you, or thank you enough, and you'll never understand me.
