# Miss You

• Miss You •

I’ve never had to feel this way
Dreams, they flash night and day
You slay you life, won’t give in to me
Someday, oh, the light you’ll see.
That you and I are meant to last
But you refuse to see that task
I prey for you to come be mine
Night and day, time after time
But deep within, I can not settle
I’ve forgotten the dreams that mettle
In and out, within and not
I want you so bad, I cannot stop
I think so much, my brain hurts
I’m bleeding slightly in the dirt.
I cannot breath without being sure
My happiness has been hidden.
