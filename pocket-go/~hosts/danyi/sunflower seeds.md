# sunflower seeds

2001 SUNFLOWER SEEDS between us..
Caged, my mind wanders around a world that slips through my fingertips, slowly decomposes itself into ashes of the floor. Tears genuine, dancing, falling from your eyes to brush upon the airs, breathe this air, inhale deeply, for it's all within you. Each drop, another ounce of hope, I can't stop crying because it feels like your fingers dig into my arm, pull me along... Forget the facade, forget forget forget all the reasoning the pleasures the lies.. forget all the past times, the current times.. forget the dreams, rumbling .. distance creeping in on you, catching your breathe and holding you tight, forever, collapsing on my lungs.. distance, stress, holding the water high and drowning me in its own sincereness. captivated, held... to home. forget forget forget not my home, to where comfort blessed me and we didn't worry.

and i'd smile, genuine, for all the ash and tears developed you. forget me nots, gardenias, and sunflower seeds.
