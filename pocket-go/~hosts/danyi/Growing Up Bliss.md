# Growing Up Bliss

2001 Accessing: Growing Up Bliss
just yesterday i'd felt so grown
stages life- where i'm alone
to make my choices, and I had seen
how easily i could be me.
i never listened, i never saw
i never noticed i wasn't grown at all
but still i thought i knew my way
the path before me left unpaved
and you were there, but now your gone
i never listened, soft hummed song
i always would argue, you never got through
and now that I've noticed, you've changed what you do
when finally i've grown some, now i'm just immature
and each moment i live, the more i grow near
confusion in understanding of how these things work
and how i could fix it to stop all this hurt
not inside me, but instead inside you.
the pain that i've caused, the words that you knew
i should have heard once, long before this
but lessons are learned through 'growing up bliss'.
