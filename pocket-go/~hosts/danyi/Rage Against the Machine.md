# Rage Against the Machine

• Rage Against The Mahine •

Burn the fire that rages inside
Like matches to the flame;
A simple question yearning deeply,
Will anything be the same?
All the fights, and memories lost
Bring me back to the shame
As I sit back, I calmly wonder
Will anything be the same?
I sat through the conflicts, worked it all out
But just when I think it’s the worst
Suddenly I struggle to understand you
And the more and more it hurts.
I made my bed and now I sleep,
But sleeping is not so good.
I read and book, and count my fears
Like a good little girl should.
One in the angers, hurts and fears
The nightmares are to blame
For all my sadness and pure madness,
Will anything be the same?
