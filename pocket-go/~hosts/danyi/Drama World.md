# Drama World

20Accessing: Drama World
Sometimes I replenish in the drama, the circles it draws around my wrists, seeming to cuff me to the ground, restraining me from being able to break down these doors and breathe the fresh air. Perhaps it's ludicrous to assume, but I feel as though nothing is phasing me, were it not for the sickened feeling that drives deep into my stomach.

I used to crave it. Sometimes.. Sometimes it seemed that life wasn't without this pain in my stomach, wasn't without these dreams being lost in a see of hate. How many times, how many goddamn times have I run form the answer only because I was scared of the change? Only because on person told me I was wrong? How many times have I bound my hands from getting what I wanted, because I thought it better not to hurt anyone else? In the mist of this drama, I hurt me. I tore me down. I made me less then what they made me. And it's hard to kick these old habits.
