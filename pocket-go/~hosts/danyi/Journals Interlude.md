# Journals Interlude

2001
Accessing: A Journal's Interlude

Often I stand up before people as if to speak, or murmur my opinion and my throat seems to seize and choke me -- a lack of air -- and for a second it is as if my heart pauses to keep me from moving and so I stop. 

The air is nice to breathe today, I'd think to myself as if the eyes that all watched me were not connected to people, and in fact there were no eyes at all. A thousand heartbeats seem to have ticked by, and yet I claim not to know exactly how long I might have stood there. Then, out of the nothingness my lungs would open up and my heart would beat, and I would speak.

I never really had anything of importance to say, at least not in my own personal opinion. I was your typically silent type of girl, who sat in the corner and simply listened, for sometimes the silent are allowed the most information. I pray often that the evil the world has gone to is not a part of me, and often I feel as though some how this all could change -- I could change. But it's a dead center path to no where when you have no fire beneath you, and often you forget the reason you even still breathe.

But this is modern society, no way to escape the chains that bind the people, and bind us more forcefully, for we are the youth, we are the unprogrammed, the rebels who were born outside the laws they'd already made, and the world had already accepted. We are the ones who sit back and we watch, for we desire this change. Would it not be so easy for them to bind us in chains, leash us like dogs to program us? Teach us to breed of the same vibrant hate they feed from? They wish only for the cycle to continue the same way. Wouldn't that be so much easier?

Hate to inform the man up there in the big chair who controls us -- and no I'm not speaking of god -- that he has no control when his time turns, when the generation turns. It's a switch grab, a hold onto the center awareness that you have no control. You live, you die. You move through it, and only have a moment to lick the very surface of all that you possess..

No one likes the taste of power, but the sick addiction is one we all know. It feels good, and it takes your mind and pounds in your head -- I am in control! I am all that had power, for these are my ruins that lay before me, these are the things I've had will to change. Praise me, they wish, for they are high standing in the eyes of the pety who think there is nothing like being up there.
