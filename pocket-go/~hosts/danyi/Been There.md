# Been There

I wish I could have been there..

Thick is the sea before us, hand in hand there is nothing but the tide to knock us down. Remember only the warm nights, when summer would wake and the clouds would be gone so all we could see were the stars.  I wish I could have been there, on these nights, when my thoughts were too lost within myself to see how blind I really was.

Balanced is the pools of water, slipping through wishes like pennies thrown away for good reason. Remember the nights where we sat facing the ocean and spoke of tomorrow, and today, and before.  I wish I could have been there, on these nights where I just hurt, because I would not let you in.

Tedious is the clock, which gently ticks a silent sound to coaxed the pain away from our hearts. Remember those sweet moments where the air tasted like candy and smelled of your perfume and the sky never seemed to question our existence. I wish I could have been there, on these nights, when my dreams were to concealed that you didn't know I cared.  

Violent are the waves that wash around our feet, the sweet caress of moments that never falter. Remember the trip we took, from here to there without knowing where we came from, or where we were going. I wish I could have been there, on these nights where I was not so easily impressed.

Perfect is the rainbow which marks the sky with a moonlight presence, touching my heart without saying goodbye. Remember the days where there was nothing but each other, and we seemed to be invisible to everything they wished to hurt us with. I wish I could have been there, on these nights when my heart was on the floor.

Angry is the laughter of the people as they point and stare, glad that they are not me. Remember the nights that the tears never stopped, and the blood kept on rushing till I couldn't breathe this high anymore. I wish you would have been there, on the night you never saw.
