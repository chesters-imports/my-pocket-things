# Dream Weaver

2001 Accessing: Dream Weaver
Raven hair adorned the morning
as he slept in silent slumber
before the dreams that came within
came screams of past held voices
past told lies played gentle melody
as the blade slide over her wrist
and while he slept, her blood did drain
into a puddle upon the floor.

She was dying, death unsettled
and yet he did not miss a beat
the dreams observed those words she'd tainted
for the love he was within.

sleep on, my darling, she did whisper
as the life drained out her wrists
the puddle deepened, dark red evening
and that was all the life she lead.
Much so worthy of more then this
to live her life no more.

Awoken wrongly the next morning
his eyes lay tightly on her body
curled within the bathroom shower
dead, no longer moving anymore
she didn't breathe, nor beat her heart
flutter her eyes open, or closed again
she was lifeless, unlike that evening
raven adorned the midnight sun.
