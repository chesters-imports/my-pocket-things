# Wishful Thinking

2000 * Wishful Thinking *
Friday, September 8th 2000 04:25AM

There's a smell in the air.
Like dew drops, it is the smell of fresh gardenias filling the winds, blissful and subtle, gentle and moving our bodies like dreams that wish they could exist.

Where is my fairytale land? Were do I want to run to, when here doesn't seem far enough away? I'm in a lapse of reasoning with myself, a wish to escape a life I've created only to embellish myself in the tiny footsteps which block the visions from seeing me so clearly.

My sister calls me a nerd. I can understand, I'm not your typical run of the mill leader, nor am I a follower. Sometimes I curiously ponder if I sit anywhere near the middle at all. Sometimes I wish she'd stop calling me that, but that's what older sisters are for. To pick on you. Hell, I do it to my little sister, so, what room to I have to complain.

But this life is so.. sweet. The way we operate, the way we move like pieces of scattered hopes, only wishing to find our way to a home lost in the collapsing flames. We rise above, for we are but phoenixes, wishing this existence upon those who seem to fair so well. Perhaps the realization that we are all fucked up, might make it easier for some of us to see inside. It's clear when you turn the lights on, when you realize my pain is yours in different words. I'm no different then any other girl my age. I do my hair a lot the same way.

I wonder if anyone reads this thing. I have to go in tomorrow to get my shots, so that I can back into school. Yes, that's right. No more high school drop out Danyi, but they all say I need those damn needles thrusted in my flesh. So, I choke back another tiny fear in hopes that perhaps the outcome won't be that bad, and I can finally move on in life.

I mean, isn't that what it's all about? Moving on, moving up? I don't mean fucking your way to the top, but it's rather simple when you live, learn, and keep living. I don't want to dwell like I used to, it's fabricated. It makes me live in an illusion that will never change, because the past never changes. Focusing on now is exciting, like a mental high that never dissipates. I wish to live in the moment forever, and smell the sweet smells. I memorize the world by smell.

Fragrant are these moments, each one ticking by like the night as I seem to miss the understanding that I have obtained. I wish to be forgiven, I wish to be trusted, I wish to be loved.

But more then those, I wish to forgive, trust, and love. Without setbacks, without well drawn out moments that caress my skin with a bitter hate towards everything, because I refuse to let them in. It's a big world out there, and my tiny life needs to experience it. No more locked inside, it's time.

On another note, Sarah is 6 days due. Yes, that's right. Little Sirena is waiting to come into this world and be blessed by all of it's gifts, it that's what you call it. If anyone is really reading this, wish her good luck for me. She's going to need it.

Now off to bed, before mom kicks my butt for being up too damned late to get up tomorrow for these evil needles.

I hope Kurr's going to be okay. -sighs-
-/danyi.
