# Hope

2001 Accessing: Hope

in the darkness, there can form only one light
soft, like the weight of a feather
drifting down from heaven to allow us time
it is fabricated, softer then silk
the touch, to roam- it is.

in the light, there can be noise
and yet the noise can be soothed
like a lion, roaring through the mountains
this is our terrain, this is the life we lead
You to your life, me to mine;
and for this moment- it is.

It is air when there was no air inside.
It was tears, when we thought we'd cried them all.
It was quiet when we prayed for peace
And joy when our hearts were lonely.
We dwell within it, depend upon it
yet it seems so thin that it cannot made
touched, or harmed.

Is it hope ..
.. and it's all that keeps us alive.
