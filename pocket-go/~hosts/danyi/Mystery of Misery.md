# Mystery of Misery

• Mystery of Misery •

Born only minutes ago,
My eyes seem broken by the pain
My ears sense the madness
My heart, only the rain.
Inside I wish I were smiling
But outside is the hate
I close my eyes and disappear
Before it came too late.
I used to be strong and hold my own
But as my life passes me by
All I have is hate within
Visions of a living lie.
Those lie of my only caring love
The words spoken through his lips
They say he is not right for me
But my memory just slips.
Now I walk away from him
Their lie oh too great
Leave and cry far away
For I have become hate.
So now he is all in pain,
And they are happy still.
Because thrashing my simple life
Is one of their many thrills.
The blankness, single dying hope
It’s too hard to understand the play
Now, next time I see my love
Together we shall stay.
