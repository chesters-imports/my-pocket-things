# Dance with me kitten

2001 Feb 24

Dance with me, kitten, a thousand years and since.. words untangled n a box of joy, a forever which lasts along this long stripe of road. The little lights glitter like the city, how the smoke boggles in the air and yet is not moving- it is here, kitten, i wish for you to dance with me. I wish for you to grasp my hands and sway, for the kitten is everything, you are everything. Silently I wait for this to change, change a million times. I wait on line for my mind to numb and for my fingers to take over. If only it was easier for me, kitten, to get you here and keep you here somewhere inside my heart. if only it was #simpliar child to not need so much to control. Control is everything, control upon myself, and on you kitten, we must learn to control this all. it's not to far off to imagine you here, it's not to long until it could be real, but I want you to dance with me, kitten. Dance with me not because you have to, or because you think it makes me feel better- dance with me because your hips sway and your mind numbs and there lie to passion. this could be yours, a thousand years and since.

a thousand years and still.
