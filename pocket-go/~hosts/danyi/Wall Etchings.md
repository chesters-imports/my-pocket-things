# Wall Etchings

1999 tender wall etchings..

God .. this is sick.. my life is sick and no-one can see me
i dance before them and they don't see me
i scream, and do they hear? They are def
And they vanish on me as quick as they came

A plea for help, i'm reaching out to grip it
Do you know that nothing is there?
when you have dug yourself down .. so fucking deep
you become the only one to unearth you.
But, how terribly i need help. i will never settle down
i will never be alive. 
that is what i think behind this optimistic face
You'll never hear me cry.

But what if indeed i want to cry out?
Cry out to every thing .. everyone that
makes me want to live.
Call out to them: Help me, dear god help me make
it to tomorrow And then i do.. and -they- don't hear.
They can't hear. My aching voice is only an illusion
dribbled in my mind.

In my head.

And they can't hear what I think.
i wouldn't let them in.. i am afraid to open to door.
So I've run myself into a circle, you say?
Tell me to my face, don't ignore me
because i am so young - don't forget me because
i was just a phase. That if i do matter, and no one sees it,
then what a waste i would be.

hell if i know who i am,
let alone anyone else know
they think they know
i think -i- know .. but no one knows
i'm carved so perfectly into the wall.
mere etchings.

Hear me .. if you hear me .. see me .. if you please
Never forget what i've asked
you are but a fucking dream i wish was true
