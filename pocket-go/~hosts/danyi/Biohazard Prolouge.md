# Biohazard Prologue

2000 Accessing: Biohazard. Prolog

Size was the only true advantage he had over her. His eyes lit like the stars, fire burned in them like angry gods, burning away at the thoughts that barely seeped into her mind. There had to be a way out of the shell, incredibly locked inside. If only she'd been a better daughter, if only she could have faced the reality of her own ignorance.
Fingers lashed skin again, his large hand wrapped around her small arms. God, she was so tiny then, there was no food to eat, there was no nothing. Her head spun in dizziness, flashing back through memory after memory. The depth of his voice paralyzed her, caused her skin to crawl.
"Again!" he shouted, roaring though clenched teeth.
