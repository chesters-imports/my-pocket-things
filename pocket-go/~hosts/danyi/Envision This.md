# Envision This

• Untitled I •

Envision This:
A lifetime of childishness
Of pure laughter
Through the rain
Where no one cares
What is said about rules
And the rules we make
 
To tie into this trial
An hour of Games,
For what we wish for
In the illusions
Of immortality.
Is what we seek
In the shelter
Of a dying
Sacred hand.
