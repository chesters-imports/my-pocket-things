# Penny

2000 Penny.

A penny sinks to the bottom of the well, soon to rust, soon to become the very waste of another 1/100 of a dollar, but I close my eyes, and I slowly wish, wish for something big, and smile, because not all dreams are true.

I'd like to fancy myself the dreamer, I look at my watch and the time ticks and drones on and on. Sometimes I wonder why the normality of this world becomes just that; the simple tick of the clock. It's so melodic, making me tired, I'm restless in my womb. Prejudged something unconcealed at birth, half of myself, I am half of myself. Is that really the truth? Can I believe the running in my head, it says your half of me, you are half of me. Broken at the womb, I lived, you died, and this is my envy, this is my sadness. Am I only half of me? Am I only and essence of two versus one, death becoming my birth? I am born like a phoenix, from my ashes, from myself. I am shying away from inhaling, inhaling sharply to remember I can breathe -- and perhaps remind myself that I should quit smoking.

The air outside is cold today, like when I'm sorrow ridden, my eyes let lose. A tear falls, crystallized to ice, marking me soulless, cold. There is nothing inside but you, nothing inside but my memories. God, I love my family, and my friends they feel like family, for without them I am shattered, broken pieces of my own frozen tear drop.

The water bubbles as the coin hits the bottom, the depth is endless in my thinking. A penny for my thoughtlessness, and eager step to rebirth. I always want to be outside myself, to see what if feels like to see me, to feel what it's like to know me. Am I only half, alone, a sea of people my only walkway? Must I pretend that I am so generous? Must you make me something more then I am? Just a patch of flowers you'd normally call weeds, plucking me from the earth -- i don't belong here. They think I just look tacky, natural state, no effort to create. Damaged, simply for a forced me.

Eyes fall upon you and lick up your essence, It's brief I make this wish of contact, I'm soaring just to see you. On a piece of parchment, on a sandy letter -- a fable was told to me, here is my wrecking ball. I wished for you because I cannot know. I feel, in a patternless hope, that something was there, behind those eyes, behind those lips... something in the smell, and I felt.. I felt I needed to inhale it again. I want to come and inhale it again.
Am I only half of me, broken at the womb?
Am I only half a dream, less then what's consumed?
Am I only eager for your dark brown eyes?
Will I only breathe allowed a risky compromise?
I want to be the tender one, a wing upon to mend
I want to be the meager one, thorough to the end
I want to bring done the stars in attempt to explain
how I am merely driven by something that's insane
I saw it in your eyes, I caught it in my breathe
And now in my sleep times drifting i rest
on this tempting delusion, this cascading dream
To dare all the urges, tempt lines unseen.

I want it to be now, the blue. I want it to cover me. I want to become the moment. I want to drift inside it all, it smells so good to be closed to you.

Am I only half of me? Sometimes I do not care.
