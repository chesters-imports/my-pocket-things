# Interruption

**An Explanation.**
  Consuming these writings may damage your mind, or piss you off, or make you wonder why they haven't locked me away in some looneybin as a nearly insane chick who might hurt someone. Who knows, those damn therapists said I was 'borderline' manic depressive, so you can't hit me up on that account. I just have mood swings.

  Imagine, a thrill of pain surging through your body, not always pain, but often just energy. It simmers inside your veins and dries out your tears and makes it nearly impossible to continue life anymore -- except unless you escape. Find a way to filter out the emotion without going insane, without hurting yourself in some way. Something that keeps the swelling down in your mind. Imagine having no where to turn, because everything you love becomes damaged with this pain that burns inside of you. It makes you tired, your eyes burn, you don't know what to do.

  I write. **Interruption** is basically a pause in my life where this pain fills my brain and I can't so anything but escape. Some of these writings have to do with people I know, but most have to do with me and my thought process. The way I deal with things. You'll probably think "Wow, this girl things bad of herself," but think of it this way: I am a typically optimistic person, who hits stages of depression that consume me. These are writings from all points of my mood swings, from wishing I was better, to knowing that I'm better, to wondering why I can't seem to better myself.

Please enjoy this little center of my mind. New things are added periodically, depending on my moods.
