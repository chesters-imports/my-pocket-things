# Second Set of Eyes

• Second Set of Eyes •

You don’t know, I’m stalking you
I see it from your point of view
You know my deepest fantasies
You are deep within me.
I understand the things you ask
You one last wish is my final task
You open your arms to comfort me
My sadness you do hate to see.
I am your sister, your answer, your kin
I’m the only one know within.
You dream of me night and day
And all the foolish games I play.
I want to be your other half,
But you won’t let me take that path.
You wish that we could simply sore
But still I need something more…
I am no longer in this game
Not knowing puts me through blame
Of how good things could be
With someone so close to me.
