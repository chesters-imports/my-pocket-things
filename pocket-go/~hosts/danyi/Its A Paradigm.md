# It's a Paradigm

2000 It's a paradigm.
Wednesday, September 6th 2000 05:56AM

I rest in my own salvation as of now, a little lucid world that seems to intertwine my emotions with my disease. I'm wearing headphones, and NIN is blaring loudly into my ears. Agro music, seems to help the pain.. to know that someone can sing your life, and not even know it.

Today was my tiny facade with myself. Actually, the last few nights have driving me insane. The other day I was up 32 hours.. I went out with Kenny, Derek, and my cousin Sam, they called my up and told me they were in town for a little while -- got into a car crash on the way up from Nocturnal Wonderland.. so they ended up having to be in town a few days to get everything worked out. Despite my beyond restlessness, I'd gone. And stayed up waay past when I should of.. But Jesus, the thoughts clog my veins and keep me reeling to know more.

So I finally slept, after some doubts of myself, of the people I knew-- a few terrible hours, but I guess that's what I get for being slightly heartless in the most caring of ways. I'd rather not explain.

Well, right now marks 22 hours of being away for today. I don't know what it is that makes me do these things to myself. It seems to... be the only way I can think. I want this change, I want to breathe it in for a moment.

Like the sweet taste of a hand's caress. I'm lost in my tiny world of sin and desire, and no one knows.

I don't want them all to know. I just want to ... keep away from those people that I love. I don't want me to be their pain anymore.

Perhaps, I'm just diseased?
-/danyi

It felt as though I'd never been kissed before. I'm just a dying rose, wilted away and useless. Who needs me? don't fool yourself.
