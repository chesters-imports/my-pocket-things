# loving you

• Loving You •

Quick, I have become a soul
In your dreams without control
Petrified of your embrace
To hold you near face to face
Close I am to words forgot
Words I fear, words I sought.
I hope to have hope without bounds
Ascending up, we’re falling down
Furnished with the purest light
Here for you to make it right
You won’t seem to go away
In my head all night and day
Once you’re mine I won’t let go
Until time, we’ll move this show
Happiness, me, you, the world
This time they can’t stop this girl
Yeah, I grow, I change sometimes
Getting better, yeah, you’re mine.
Mine, oh my mine without doubt
Sane is sane when not allowed
I feel so close to you each night
Like link together, send away the fright.
Feeble I have become in you
Doing whatever you tell me too
And if I disagree you won’t mind
We shall stand the test of time
For you and me were meant to be
If not forever, then we will see
I will not end it because of the length
Distance makes a hearts strength.
I love you. There, I said it now
I dream to dream, that is my vow
For as long as we will last
Always first, never last.
Take me by the hand and lead
But never forget to take care of me
I am fragile as the wind is clear
And loosing you is my biggest fear.
