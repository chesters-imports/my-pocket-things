# Dancing Heartache II

2001 Accessing: Dancing Heartache II
often it seems happiness comes within dreams
that escape as i lay my head down, now i sleep
tomorrow is dawning and my fears keep on yawning
they scream at me lies of defeat, I now seep
how hard i hung on, because i thought we were strong
like the riddles would sing on forever, for ages
and now that your gone, i can't break free of the song
and all of the storyless pages.

you'd follow me down, forward, back and around
and yet now you've parted your lies, these hopes
you dream up at night words built out of fright
and don't give yourself time to see, to cope
the ways things are seeming, we must have been dreaming
to make this dream last this long, so long
tomorrow was raging with hatred that stranger
then all of the words in the songs
we have given up this fight-
scared of the nights after we'd parted are ways
the cool summers night with no one in sight
no one to hold us so close.
I inhaled only you, my sweetest of drugs,
and now the days we have killed
no moment to question life's daily lesson
all that was said is don't write.
