# Christopher Robin

2001 Accessing: Christopher Robin
I remember briefly the charm. 

My mind almost numbs to remember, time and time has passed. But I still remember you, as I remember everyone that comes and goes in my life that holds any importance. I remember my vain errors, as well as yours, and a distant compromise that has led to my somehow loss of you.

Christopher Smith, indeed it was. You were born in Spokane, Washington, or at least that is where you lived when I first started talking to you. Contact always seemed to be lost between us, but it was no feat to find it again. We talked and talk, and god, I must have known you are year before you finally needed a place to go -- and I said, come in, come in.. My door is always open.

Time ticks and on and on, time always seems to throw the world out of balance. I was involved, and I was superior, blind, and most weary of my own actions. I created my own demise, as well as yours, in leaving. It's been a while, shouldn't been this long.

You left. I do believe it was raining that night, and your hair seemed to mat to your face. "One more night, just one more," you pleaded. I think we bargained on that one last night, and then you were gone. Gone, and I wasn't sure how to feel.

And time past. You contacted me after a while, in silly rhyme that nearly scared the shit out of me, but I'd figured out who you were. You said you wanted to keep in touch, but I've always been bad about replying to emails.

Time passes. And continues to pass. I figure, sometimes it's okay to let go and never remember, but this is one of those things I cannot forget. If you ever -- ever read this, and are out there Chris, remember all the fun times, and fucking keep in touch.

Because despite the changes we see and feel, we are always connected in this tangled web.
