# Forget Me Knots

2001 
forget me knots-- and lullabies-
tranquil sighs within your eyes-
dreams of times which pass us by
and paralyze this moment's cry.
licking scabs within her dreams-
nothing will be more then it seems-
and gentle are the distant screams
of many things we wish to deem
important to the soul's content-
to whisper words in which we meant-
things they'll label heaven sent
words that come- from where they went
and inside are these sardonic cries-
tears which sac-ri-fice your eyes-
words that whisper tattered lies-
too soothe the pain bared inside.
