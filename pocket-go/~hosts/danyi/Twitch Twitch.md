# Twitch Twitch

1999 Twitch, twitch...

I am confused right now. I am in pain. I can barely concentrate. Why do things have to be they way they are? I only want to hold someone, touch them, makes the simple looks into their eyes. And I am powerless to receive anything like this, so instead I sit here and I type.

I want to make it all better, I was to clean the wounds once began by other people, but so much more harshly inflicted by me.

I have a bad habit. I like to get close, as close as touch, to most people I know. There is a side there that no one normally sees without closeness. No one understands why I like to do this, but I do. I think it's because I never have anything pure, anything that lasts, so I am so used to always looking for something.. a touch.. There is so much inside a touch.

So, only now I just sit with my fingers pressing numbly against the keys of this keyboard, my eyes watching gently as the world moves behind me and I don't even notice the time ticking by. This is me, this is me. I am oh so fucking alone, and it hurts. I want to smile, I want to feel that twist in my stomach, that knot of nervousness, the thought of something coming that is unexpected, but when time moves by without notice, nothing is unexpected, nothing is expected. It's a mandatory void, a moment's breath of nothingness that could be so much more passionately spent upon someone else. The sharing of attention, the beating of an excited heart.

But I am just alone.
