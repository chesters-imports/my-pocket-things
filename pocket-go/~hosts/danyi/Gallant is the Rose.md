# Gallant is the Rose

2001 Accessing: Gallant is the Rose
gallant is the rose- which left me undisguised
with words upon my lips, prose reflected in your eyes
a thousand miles deeper- to where i cannot turn
aching in this void of world, the words- we let them burn
to whisper in a tiny rhyme, to you- i'd kiss your heart
to where i have not begun, but indeed where i will start
and slipping through the serene seas- hidden in this lust
it's a forever in you i now fear- a lack luster to my trust
if i cannot trust me, my sir- then how can I trust you?
drops and holes and threads of life- forward this to you
forever dances madly- i can see far past these eyes
and what can see inside, we could hardly compromise
it is a feeling- a color- a smell, it lacks not hope
where the sparrows chirp- a plausible need to cope
a care of second chances- breaking from a shell
while this i am forever, this will be my lying hell
and we could dance forever, if only i trusted me
where loss is overwhelming, and hate is all I see.
