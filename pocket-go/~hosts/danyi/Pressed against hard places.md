# Pressed against hard places

Pressed against hard places
My body against the holy edge
I am the taste of ecstacy in flesh
Mouth of god pressed against yours
Open for the annihilation
Of what never suited you anyways
Be made mine under the wait of
Wet desire, waves crashing against 
Your skin is etched in the flames of my longing
Your neck engulfed in the teeth of my need
I taste you like ripe fruit, juice pouring
And slide my soul into yours again
