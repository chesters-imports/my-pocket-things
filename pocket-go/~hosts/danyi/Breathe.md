# Breathe

2001 Accessing: Breathe
Breathe in. There is a slow draw to your voice, which never existed far outside of my head. A mutter, and I'm drained, with the exception of my eyes, which now glow over you. I can see, see before me, but nearly as much as I wish I could have seen before all this world was came tumbling down in front of me. How many things have we sacrificed? A small trade for a bit of help. A bit of help because we want more. Nothing is good enough, flesh eating the flesh of my mind -- it's clear inside here, we dwell and inhale to save our very nature. Never forgotten, those are our pleas. 

Gracious, now the clouds quilt my arms, and I am blanketed in an insecure warmth. I hate to be alone, but I always run away. Modest are my capabilities to understand my own inadequacies I have with myself. It's a drama loop, and it's hand created. It's it my right, it is in my blood, but I put it there. I carved a hole far deeper into my heart then the mind would allow. Scars on the tissue, my soul is battered.
