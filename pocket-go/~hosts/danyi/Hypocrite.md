# Hypocrite

2001 Accessing: I am a Hypocrite
Content in breathe, shallow to inhale, I am eye sight often when you are blind, a shoulder to lean off on, a crutch to keep you sane. Within a breath, I am magnified. I am the clouds, the promise, the hope. My eyes are soft to emotion, for emotion is what layer of dust I've dwelt upon. I will never cause you harm, for I could not damage the lifelines within this bed of roses. My fingers are nimble, touch you softly, the world is utterly delicate.

I am flawed. Creation is my disaster. Each thing I touch is broken beneath my fingertips, for I have become the hate of society. I am greed, I lust in nature, and thrive off attention. I am narrow minded, although I seem to pretend I can be a little more flexible. I stick to my traits, I'm not easily changed. I can be the fear, the hate, and the drama of all that is your existence.

Snowflake, not one alike. My eyes seem to focus higher, on a goal perhaps unachieved. I wish only the best for you, and will choke down the pain to make it all work. I am forgiving, often to much for my own good. I'm easily walked on, often used, and my means seem to bastardize themselves without help at all. I'm stigma, something to keep the blood flowing. You only want this kiss.

Pure, without scratches. That's all I've ever wanted. I want to lift each person I know, and smile because I know they've been well. I neglect myself, walk on myself, use myself. I am more abusive towards me then you could even imagine. I am the society I hate, the change in the contract, the words that seem so out of place. I'm the strand of hair that I see as being wrong, and yet I do not wish to change. I am selfish, childish, needy. I am the greed inside your heart.

I'm affectionate, I like to be paid attention to. I like to give my attention to others. I grow bored easily, and let my heart run away with a passing feeling. I am too deep, I crave too much meaning from each thing as it happens. I believe in fate, and yet wonder why time ticks and fails me every time. I want to much from you, and you will soon want nothing of me.

I am freedom to express yourself, art where there is no inspiration. I am patternless, and hardly understood. I am lost even when you are found.

I am your dream, and I am your nightmare.
Content in breathe, shallow to inhale.

Hypocrite.
