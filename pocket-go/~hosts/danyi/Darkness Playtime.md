# Darkness Playtime

• Darkness Playtime •

Darkness envelops
Removing a piece of my madness
Touching it,
Caressing it like it is it’s own.
The pure terror breaks me down
I diminish into your arms
Arms that used to bring me happiness
Arms that only bring me pain.

And everything goes black

Shapes spin and dance in the blankness
Blinking, rotating
I’m going insane.
And through the silence,
Voices come, echoing
Telling me lies
Mixing it deep within bitterness

I scream, and nothing is there

I wake again
Upon my bed
Tears dripping down my face
It was all I dream, I whispered
As I left to a saner place.
