# A Sacred Hand You Seek

• Untitled II •

A sacred hand you seek, I see
To help you run away.
Immortality ain’t true
That’s the least I’ll say.
I’m going to tell you of a way
To get away from life
Grab a gun and kill yourself
Well, isn’t that nice?
Oh, remember when you cock the gun
Your killing someone loved
And perhaps they call it suicide,
But that’s not what I think of.
It’s first degree murder, and selfish at that
Cause now many people are hurt.
Hurt because you were to weak
To conquer this pile of dirt.
You are the loser,
Now that your dead
Shot away live
Right through the head
And I never warned you
Just told you these lies
About lifes best pleasures
When you say good-bye.
