# Fuse This

2001 Accessing: Fuse This
Fuse the mind, I'm paralyzed by this ecstacy. To know, to know I am alive and that I can breathe and become the movement of everything that is around. Perhaps not everything, but I am held to the light, far away, tested, judged. What am I? Who am I? Falling like snowflakes, I'll only return to the sky once I melt.

Fire is my ignition, driving insanity, driving my fire forever. I don't know the road before me -- I know I've been in error.. I know I might still need change, subtle is the winds, please let the road guide me. The sun shines brightly in my face, washing away the tears, washing away your hate. i grow from this, the fuel you feed me. you are my passion, my forever, the loop. You are all that I wish to understand, and more. and the fact is, you don't even know your name. you don't even know I'm speaking of you, for you'd never divulge your eyes into this -- or perhaps you would? I always assumed you wouldn't care, it's teasing nonsense on the tip of my tongue, still licking my lips from that one moment.

it was divine wine in its taste, and I am blessed to surpass it's meaning in thought, dwelling and thinking of the pas and all the things that I have been through. I remember so many faces I cannot grasp, I do not know anymore. I'm sad to know so many people had gone on and forgotten, that so many people may have loathed me the way they did.

I was a silly little child then, now I think of your eyes, and his eyes, and I blink slowly and inhale, because I don't know the next step, I truly can't say where I'm heading. Often, the most I do is based on pure instinct, this is the way that I am, this is what I feel I must do.

Jealous I am of the stars, they have a place, they know what they do. They bring light in the darkness, and illuminate all of the hope we have in the world. 

I, on the other hand, wander.
