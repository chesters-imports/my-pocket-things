# Dancing Heartache

Accessing: Dancing Heartache I
In these dreams it never hurt this bad
a breathe inhaled stings the blackened heart
my world is void around me, collapsing on my lungs
lungs which cannot suffer to breathe anymore.
my tears are just Satan's satisfaction, that he took my dreams
and formed them up, and damned them to crash around me
a facade of high hopes, stale words that mock my lips
I'm intangible now, death has become me, but only until i wake
only until this dream dies.

how to escape the loop, nevertheless, it is nothing i run from
a disease fibbed, a lie spread across pieces of bread which will never be eaten
you smile, and go, you lie, and go.. There is much more for you to do here
Much more in life then to spend your time watching me
Watching the tears stain my cheeks,
my breathe barely able to collapse into my lungs.
You cannot hold me in your arms anymore.

Had I only seen, had I only been less selfish..
this is that, I wanted your happiness, and I let you run for it,
I watch you run for it, for there is nothing more important..
But to be there, to watch you look over at me and smile, even once more
the distance is depth, and you lack of words are the answer
the go ahead on the stories I've made, the false lies tangled in my head.
'it is a tangle web we weave,' and I cannot run away..
I swore upon my hatred that it would not happen anymore
and I swear this on your love.
Happiness will keep us together.
