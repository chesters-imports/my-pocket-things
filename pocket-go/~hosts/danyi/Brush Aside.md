# Brush Aside

2001 Accessing: Brush Aside
In this illusion, I am never cast aside
never thrown away..
my world is a cave that seems to engulf me
test me, because i need to be sure
sure that those precious hands were not a facade
that when i awoke, the dream wasn't really a dream
I'd cry for you, if it would make the answers clearer
I would scream to god, if only i knew he heard
for you made me smile
and my tears were not as painful

In this dream, I'm never let down
the air in my lungs is cleaner then it ever was before
like i'd never learned to breathe
and now the air tasted of the morning
my eyes close and i can still see
for my world has yet to die

In this dream, we are forever
because there could be no other
no smile could feel as good as it crossed my lips
to cross my eyes

that same smile i see in you
as you brush the tears aside
for this is much more important.
