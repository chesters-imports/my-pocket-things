# Broken Happiness

2001 Accessing: Broken Happiness
Tainted and losing, I fall to t he ground
Echoed and soundless, I undo what's bound
By a simple perplextion I happened to miss
A breath caught within an illusional kiss.

Tomorrow's injustice is bringing me down
Envy soft spoken, I firmly now drown
To a bottomless ocean of torment and dreams
This complex devotion must be more then I see

Now broken and bruised I herein carry on
To a mist of the future; the medowlark's song.
