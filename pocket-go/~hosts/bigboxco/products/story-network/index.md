# Boxing a story network

{{div:.bbc-lede}}
What if Big Box’s office suite boxed something else — a show, or a network of toys and systems that all tell one greater story? Catalog the parts, track what happens when, and plan the release order of information without inventing a brand-new stack from scratch.
{{/div}}
{{/div}}

**Status: concept / something to consider.** Not a product SKU. A map of how existing (and coming-soon) desks might share the load.

## Where the story begins (internal)

Kitten’s start point: **`go.rituals/mirror-box`** — Misery’s Mirror Box opener (“Misery Becomes Me”).

**Release sort:** that prose is **private / never leave** as public packaging. It may anchor the network **inside** the pocket. Do not auto-ship it to X or external channels.

## The desire

Lots of moving parts. Hard to catalog. Hard to track which things happen when. Hard to know the **release order of information** — what the audience (or the toys) are allowed to know, and in what sequence. Day-job Big Box tools already solve cousins of those pains.
