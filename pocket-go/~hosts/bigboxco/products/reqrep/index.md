# ReqRep

{{div:.bbc-lede}}
A request bay for product asks. Talk in blocks, seal what is true, lock the purpose, then sign a Product prep before anyone builds. Only signed prep leaves the bay as law.

{{div:.bbc-pillars}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**BLOCKS**
Numbered discussion. Threads expand. Speak as you or as the desk assistant. Read first; forms open when you choose.
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**AGREED**
You close the block when it is settled. Purpose gets written, then locked. Unlock is a process, not a silent edit.
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**SIGNED PREP**
A sibling artifact, not a stripped comment thread. Unsigned prep is not law. First slice waits for your stamp.
{{/div}}
{{/div}}


People call it RepReq when they are moving fast. The chip on the ROM is **ReqRep**. Same bay. IBM Plex. Gold. Not pocket cloth. Not grey admin.

This is where a product ask stops being a hallway conversation. Numbered blocks hold the talk. You seal what is agreed. Purpose freezes. A second artifact — **Product prep** — is the only thing allowed to walk out.
