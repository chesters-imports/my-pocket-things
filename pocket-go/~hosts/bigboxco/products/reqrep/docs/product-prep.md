# Product prep

## What it is

**Product prep** is a second document on the case — a clean handoff written for build work. It is not the discussion thread with the comments deleted. It is a sibling artifact.

After purpose is locked and the important tickets are settled, the desk can **generate** a prep body from the material on the case. In the current slice, generate uses a **local template**, not a large-language-model rewrite. You can edit the body, save it, and then sign it.

## Why it exists

Discussion is for figuring things out. Prep is for leaving the bay with a contract.

If people build from open comments, the working relationship breaks in predictable ways: scope drift, half-remembered agreements, “I thought we already decided.” ReqRep makes the gate explicit. First-slice work should follow **signed** Product prep.

## How to produce it

1. Settle discussion. Seal the tickets that are actually agreed.
2. Write purpose until it reads true, then **lock** it.
3. In the Product prep dock, **Generate**.
4. Read the result. Edit anything that is wrong or thin. **Save**.
5. When it is right, **Sign**.

After sign, the desk shows that prep is signed leave-bay law. Treat it that way.

## What signing means

Signing is your acceptance that this document is the build contract for the first slice. It is a case-level seal, different from sealing one discussion ticket.

Until you sign:

- Prep may still be edited
- It is not the leave-bay contract

After you sign:

- Prep is the document that may leave the bay
- Discussion and locked purpose remain archive around it

## Relationship to older house patterns

Inside Big Box there are older markdown planner patterns (structured talk, freeze, receipts). ReqRep turns the useful part of that pattern into product UI: clear speakers, open versus closed blocks, and a frozen sibling writeup. You do not need to know those older files to use ReqRep. This page is enough.
