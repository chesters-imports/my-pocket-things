# Narrative planner

**Something to consider — a use of the request bay, not a new SKU.** ReqRep is where a product ask stops being hallway mush: numbered talk, seal what is true, lock purpose, sign prep before anyone builds. That same shape can hold **narrative planning** talk.

## Why this fits

On the other side of day-job work there may be a desire for a show — or a **network of toys and systems** that tell one greater story. That has moving parts, arguments about canon, and decisions that must become law before someone builds a prop, a page, or a release.

ReqRep’s job in that world:

- Open a request for a story decision, arc beat, or world rule under debate
- Discuss in blocks until the room can seal what is agreed
- Lock purpose (“what this decision is for”)
- Sign a prep-style handoff when the decision is allowed to leave the bay and drive builds elsewhere

You are not asking ReqRep to be the story bible. You are asking it to be the **discussion and seal desk** so narrative law is not stuck in chat heat.

## How you might use it

| ReqRep piece | Narrative reading |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Request | “Decide reveal order for X,” “Settle whether Y is canon,” “Approve season theme” |
| Blocks | Arguments, references, options |
| Agreed / sealed | What the room holds as true for now |
| Purpose lock | Why this decision exists (audience, toy line, continuity) |
| Signed prep | Handoff that GRM, sopr, or a builder may treat as law |

## Pairing with the rest of the suite

- **ReqRep** — talk and seal narrative decisions
- **Great Road Mapper** — when units ship / when information may release (see storyline productions on GRM)
- **sopr Documenter** — compile fragmented lore and planning scraps into an honest outline

Together they are one way to **box something else**: a story network, not only Alice Box day-job desks.

## What this is not

- Not a live “Narrative mode” toggle in ReqRep
- Not a claim that Product prep is renamed Story bible
- Not a substitute for cataloging every toy and system (that catalog is still an open desire)
