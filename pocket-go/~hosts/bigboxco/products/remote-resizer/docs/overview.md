# Overview

## What Remote Resizer is

**Remote Resizer** is a planned Big Box product for marketing artists and marketeers who must ship the same game creative across a long list of banner and button sizes.

You connect an **AI API you are allowed to use**, supply a **square master** and a **wide master**, provide a **formatted list of target sizes**, and **run**. The desk returns the converted banner set.

Product code (proposed): `CO.BBC-004-RRZ`. Folder: `remote-resizer`. Status: **coming soon**.

## The problem it solves

Per-game promo packs already demand dozens of width×height pairs. The house inventory under the-marketeers is real — unique sizes climb past eighty after dedupe. Doing that by hand is cognitively and time-hostile: same focal read, status, logo, and CTA must survive tiny crops and odd aspect ratios without you rebuilding composition eighty times.

Remote Resizer is meant to take the masters + the list and produce the set, so the human reviews and fixes exceptions instead of originating every size from scratch.

## Who it is for

Marketing Art / marketeers who own banner and button packs. Not a general photo resizer toy — a house desk aimed at game promo composition reality.

## What is not decided yet

- Whether best practices live as **configurable layouts** you edit, or as **taught rules** the model carries, or both
- Exact export formats and pack folder layout
- How tightly it binds to the official size master list vs a freeform paste list
