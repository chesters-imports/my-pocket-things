# Bug Catcher

{{div:.bbc-lede}}
Give testers a simple place to catch bugs — type them in, drop them in bins — then export a pack that imports cleanly into Jira or whatever bug tracker the house uses. Also heard as BugBox or Bud.
{{/div}}
{{/div}}

**Status: coming soon / early idea.** Not developed as a live desk yet. This landing captures the product intent so the bay is honest about what Bug Catcher is for.

The pain is the handoff: testers can find issues faster than a ticket system wants them typed. Bug Catcher is meant to be the easy catcher on their side; you collect their exports and pour them into Jira (or another tracker) without retyping the whole mess.

{{div:.bbc-pillars}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**CATCH**
Testers type bugs into a low-friction bin — not a full Jira form on day one.
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**BIN**
Organize what was caught (severity, area, build — exact fields still open).
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**EXPORT**
Output shaped for import into Jira or other management software. Testers send you the file; you load the tracker.
{{/div}}
