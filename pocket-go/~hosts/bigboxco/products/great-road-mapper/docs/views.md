# Views

All main views share the same filter rails (line, lane, lifecycle, quarter). Clicking a title opens the same drawer.

## Board

Pipeline columns by schedule lane (derived from phase dates). Cards show code, name, phase, crew cues, and twin badges. Empty clean surface invites **+ Title**.

## List

Default tab. Sortable table of titles with quarter, phase, twin, and related columns. Click a row to open the title.

## Gantt

Read-only paint of dated phases and workstreams: bars, gate diamonds, ship tick. Same filters. Click a bar or label to open the title. Collapse controls hide clutter per title. If nothing is dated yet, set ship dates / schedule bins first.

## By quarter

Titles grouped into quarter strips. Useful when the slate spans more than one board column story.

## Phases & gates

Shared vocabulary for phase and gate labels used across the house models — the legend behind the spines.

## People

House roster and assignment views — who exists to pin, and where they are pinned.

## Configurations

Product type / model configurations — the templates that mint an empty DOS spine when you create a title.
