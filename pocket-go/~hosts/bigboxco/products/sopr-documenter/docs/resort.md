# Resort

Resort is the product, not a side feature.

## Open kanban

**View → Resort (kanban)** or **Ctrl+2**.

Columns are sections. Cards are parts. Drag between columns or reorder within a column. Section outline order can move too (Ctrl+↑ / Ctrl+↓ on the active section in document mode).

## What never happens on a move

Part codes do **not** rename. `SPR-0007` stays `SPR-0007` whether it lives under "Problem" or "Ship notes." Only membership and order change.

That rule is why resort is thinking instead of bookkeeping.

## When to resort

- Headers that made sense at intake no longer match the story
- Two sections want to merge or split
- Print / reader still feels dishonest
- You are ready to pretend you planned it (marketing-approved joke; still true)

## After resort

Flip to **Print / reader** (Ctrl+3). If the TOC and body feel true, you have a document shape. If not, back to kanban.
