# sopr Documenter

{{div:.bbc-lede}}
For all your very hard to compile documentation needs. You do not outline first. You dump scraps under section bins, resort until the structure is honest, then read the document you actually meant.

{{div:.bbc-pillars}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**DUMP**
Fragments land under a section. Text, image, or table. Composer stays at the top of the active bucket; stored scraps stack below.
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**RESORT**
Kanban columns are sections. Cards are parts with stable codes. Move and reorder freely — IDs never rename.
{{/div}}
{{div:.bbc-pillar}}
**READ**
Print / reader shows the honest outline. Loose / unbinned intake stays out of the print body until you place it.
{{/div}}
{{/div}}

People say **sofr** when dictation gets cute. The product chip is **sopr**. Same desk. Big Box Company. Boring on purpose — documentation industrial, not a lifestyle brand.

This is pre-doc structure: **section bins + ordered fragments → resort → outline → exportable document.** Intake order is not final outline. That is the whole point.
