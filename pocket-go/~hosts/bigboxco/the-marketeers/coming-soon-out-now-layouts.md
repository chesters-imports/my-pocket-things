# Coming Soon / Out Now — layout frameworks

Website family: **320×150 · 1024×272 · 1320×358 · 1920×510**  
Fonts: **Anton** (status) · **Barlow Black** (title) · Barlow for support  
Same skeleton every size — scale, don’t invent a new layout. Same left/right for Coming Soon and Out Now; only change status words.

## Team fails these frameworks force against
- Logos too small
- Logos pressed against character necklines / faces
- Backgrounds not scaled or placed
- FX too big or too small

## Target pattern (from Betsoft.com + competitor scan)
- Status as a **compact badge** (OUT NOW / COMING SOON), not part of the game logo
- **Quiet left copy lane** (darkened / low-detail)
- **Character / key art on the right**
- **Protected logo** — exclusion zone around face/neck; logo readable, not tiny, not on the torso crop
- **One CTA** if needed; never two equal buttons on compact ads
- FX mid-scale inside the art — never competing with title/logo

## Size notes
### 320×150
Ruthless: status + title/logo + art (+ one CTA max). Logo roughly **30–42% width**. Single character crop. Almost no FX particles.

### 1024×272 / 1320×358
Copy left ~30–34% · art right. Short support line OK. Keep **6–8% bottom buffer** so logo isn’t cut.

### 1920×510
Cinematic OK; keep critical copy/logo inside central **~80%** width (partners trim edges). Status can sit top-right badge; title still dominant.

## Artist place-here (verbal)
1. Status in the badge  
2. Game title under it  
3. Fit logo in the LOGO box — not tiny, not on the neck  
4. Character/key art in the art zone  
5. FX mid-size inside the art  
6. One button only · Out Now vs Coming Soon = badge words only
