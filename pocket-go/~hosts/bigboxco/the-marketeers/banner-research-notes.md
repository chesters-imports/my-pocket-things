# Banner research notes

Games are sold by the banner/button. Frameworks should match what actually sells.

## What works
- Single unambiguous focal read (title → status → CTA)
- Character framing, not clutter
- Low-detail copy lane so type survives small crops
- Status as separate signal from the game logo
- One stable CTA endpoint
- Logo contrast independent of busy backgrounds
- Card grids with consistent title bands (Hacksaw)

## Avoid
- Logo colliding with neck / face / key prop (Betsoft Good Girl detail hero = memorable but risky for short banners)
- FX competing with title
- Title/logo only in a croppable edge
- Multiple equal CTAs on compact ads
- Copy over faces / high-frequency FX without a scrub
- Status floating with no hierarchy

## QA checklist
- At 320×150: status, title/logo, CTA readable in under two seconds?
- Logo touching neck/face/hand/bright FX?
- Background quieter behind every text block?
- Logo complete after ~5–8% edge crop?
- Exactly one primary action on compact sizes?
- Coming Soon / Out Now consistent and subordinate to title?
