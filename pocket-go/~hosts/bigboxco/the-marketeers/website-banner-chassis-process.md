# Website banner chassis process

Agreed in the marketing meeting **2026-09-15**.

**Why this exists:** Coming Soon / Out Now (and other site chrome) covers part of the hero and feature promo banners. If management reviews the bare art, they see "empty space" that the chassis will actually fill. Today we got that exact feedback because a banner was shown outside the website frame.

**Rule:** never send management bare hero / feature promo banners that are designed to sit under overlays. Approve inside the site chassis first.

Covers both:

- **Hero banner**
- **Feature promo banner**

Related craft notes (composition, not this approval path): [[coming-soon-out-now-layouts]].

---

## Flow

### 1. Design static
Marketing Art designs the Coming Soon / Out Now art as **static** for hero and feature promo.

Design for the covered zones. Assume Out Now, Coming Soon, or nothing may sit on top depending on status. Do not compose as if the full rectangle stays empty forever.

### 2. Hand to Alan (web)
Give Alan the static assets. He installs them on the **testing / mockup website**.

### 3. Review in chassis
Open the mockup and check the banner with:

- **Out Now** covering
- **Coming Soon** covering
- **Nothing** covering

Judge composition, empty space, logo/character survival, and status badge collision **inside the real frame**, not in isolation.

### 4. Marketing Art approval (Kitten)
Approve (or send back) while it is still in the mockup site.

### 5. Management final
Only after chassis approval: send the **in-chassis examples** (or screens of them) to management for final approval.

---

## Do / don't

**Do**
- Design static first, then prove it in the site
- Review Out Now / Coming Soon / none before calling it done
- Show management what the player will actually see in the chassis

**Don't**
- Send bare heroes meant for overlay to management
- Treat "empty space" feedback from an out-of-chassis review as gospel without re-checking in frame
- Skip Alan's install step to save time
