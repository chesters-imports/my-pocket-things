# Recurring

Hard musts also sit on Google Calendar (primary) with phone popups. Soft loops stay here.

## Weekly musts (calendar)

- [<] **Monday EOD** — order CookUnity (5:00 PM ET · popup 60m / 15m)
- [<] **Tuesday early** — laundry basket out (6:45 AM ET · popup night-before + 30m)
- [<] **Thursday EOD** — trash cans to curb (5:00 PM ET · popup 60m / 15m)
- [<] **Every other Thursday EOD** — recycling to curb (same 5:00 window · next 2026-10-02 · then biweekly; bins fill slow so trust the calendar)

## Daily-ish (soft)

- [.] water
- [.] outside (even a porch minute) — also invited on calendar 4:30–5:15
- [.] eat (name it when it happens → [[done]])

## Rule of the sweet chap

Musts get a clock. Soft loops get a nudge. Missed one? Mark `[>]` if it moves, `[x]` when it lands on [[done]], keep the clip bright — never sad.
