# {{title}}

~She cannot remember anything but having fallen here, the wounded breaking against the endless cascade. The walls connected through and around her, making her skin, her bone, her wet and needy. The ground was a weight tied to her mind, keeping her down, down here... whereever here was. Her skin is pale and naked, tingling with arrival and the sense of something calling her beyond. Her belly aches with need, with direction. What was it she needed again? She stands, naked struck against the moonlight. In the distance, there is a light. A small sign. A place to arrive at. She looks down at her bare flesh. A shrug, she's been in more awkward states before.~

~He should not be looking. That is the first truth. The second is that he already is — stopped between the trunks where the moonlight cuts a path she does not know she has walked into. Pale. Bare. Wet with arrival. A fallen thing that shrugs like this is ordinary. He stays in the dark of the trees so she can keep the shrug, so he does not startle the need in her belly or the light she is already turning toward. He watches the ache of her. He does not call out. He lets her be seen.~

~She could feel something watching, a presence. It made the ache in her form even more. She wasn't sure what it was, this new place, this new form.~
