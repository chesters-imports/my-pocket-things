### THE CASE TURNS

{{div:casecard}}
I can't focus.

He is too attractive. 

I think he is talking to me.

He runs his hand through his hair.

He passes me his card.

There are letters I know, but I cannot focus.

"You smell like her, you know?" I think he says.

I suddenly feel exposed, like I am wearing nothing at all.

"Are you okay? You look flush." he whispers. 

My body aches. I try to look up at him, but no one is there.

I remember something about sandwiches. And rockets.

And desire.

There is a smell of frankincense and myrrh in the air. 
The ground is rain and sandalwood.

A bottle of oil remains.

The label says "Wear me."

I seem to be wearing nothing at all.
