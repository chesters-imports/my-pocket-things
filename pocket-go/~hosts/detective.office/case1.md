### THE CASE TURNS
{{div:casecard}}
  It is another Tuesday on Case Street.

  Another case arrives like a stack of papers between panes of glass.

  A woman enters, tall and slender. A ghost of herself, or perhaps she'd seen one.

  I inquire what brings her to the office.

  "The girl," she says. "I have a daughter. She has been acting strangely."

  "I heard you were the kind of person to talk to about this," she continued.

  I didn't know what that could mean, but I nodded along. I usually nod along. I never know what anything means.

  "Yes, I am sure I can. I usually do," I pitch. I do not even know if that is factual. How did I get here? What is this place?

  "She keeps... saying the wrong things." The woman continued.

  I gulp. Do I say the wrong things? Who have I been talking to.

  "Go on," I reply. "Wrong in which fashion?"

  "Well," and now the gulp seems worn by the mother. "Things she shouldn't know. Past things. Future things."

  There is a long space of silence between us. Or maybe it is only half a second. Is time moving?

  "*Wrong* things." The final words are crisp with certainty.

  "I will look into it." I reply. She slides me a card.

```card
Mary Mayweather
Therapist
RTM-02CGPT-GLS
```

  And an envelope. The label says **Incidents**. I hold it in my hand tightly.

  "Call me as soon as you realize anything," she says and leaves just as quickly as she can.

  I intend to call.
  I always intend to call.

  Was that even a phone number my phone can call?
