## {{deck}}
**#Lindsey**

I have packed my bags.

 I am not sure where I am going yet but I feel bound towards the travel. An eternity has past here, even if it’s only been 2 years and my need to escape has been a growing howl In my ear. I admit I ignored it for most of the time, labeled it anxiety or laziness. Of course I wanted to run away, no one wants to work a dead end job. No one else wants to be a dead end person. No one wants to face their bad mistakes, even if that is only to point the blame at a catalyst most could agree had been detrimental. 

My therapist has said to me, more than once I am sure, *Lindsey you are depressed. Of course the idea of a new life sounds good. Maybe you need a vacation. *

*Too hard on myself and yet when I lessen my grip....*

In fact that’s what I told everyone this was. I bought a round trip ticket to Bulgaria for a two week vacation. Everyone seemed happy at work and my therapist thinks it’s a good bit of self-care. 

I have no plans to come home. 

**#Marloes**
---
It’s not every moment in life we get a chance like this. A moment to step outside of the normal and to experience something grand. You can’t bring much but what could you even really need that you can’t get once you get to where you’re going? A few basics is all I would need. 

Mama always said she wished I could travel the world. I think she meant beach side vacations in Mexico or The Bahamas, maybe backpacking for months in France or London, somewhere expected. 

Maybe she didn’t expect it to be without a scheduled end. Maybe she expected that after some jaunty visits to a few good pubs, and after a couple one night stands and an experimentative night with cocaine, I would return home. 

But mama also says, beggars can’t be choosers. Who could pass up a chance like this to leave it all behind?

**#Kevin**
---
I am lost. I have been lost. Lost on the road, lost in my own life. She was all that mattered and now she was gone and it didn’t really matter the circumstances around it, I’ve decided, just the fact that she isn’t there when I wake up in the morning is enough to make me drink. Drink and drink. Drink is the only solution. Drink is what keeps me centered. 

I found my car keys. I need to go on a drive. I can’t be here in this room anymore with the empty pillows and the cold, empty bed. I need something else. I need somewhere else. 

Why did God do this to me? Is that even a fair question to have? I do t really care anymore because it’s the question I most want to shout, the question I want to scream from the highest mountains. How can this beautiful treasure be taken from me, from this world? How can there be a caring God and have this hallow space left where there was once something flawless and holy itself. 

No god at all, that I can feel certain of. Just a waste of atoms thinking they matter. We are laughable. That much I know now. Absolutely laughable and I am going to get the last laugh. 

Or the last drink. 

Where are my keys again?
