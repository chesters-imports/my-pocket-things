# Hives in the Dark

Late. Living alone with Dad. He was already asleep.

The whole back of my legs and my butt erupted in hives. I did not know what they were. I was terrified. About twelve.

I tried to wake him. He told me to go back to sleep — maybe something got on my skin.

I still do not know what it was. Other hives came later. That night was one of the worst.

Caught again years later, dozing, an itch on the back of the leg — the same weather walking back in.
